**Settings ▸ Downloads ▸ Plug-ins** lists open-source VST3 plug-ins from the
[Nota plug-in registry](https://github.com/nota-daw/nota-plugins-registry) — synths like Surge XT and
Dexed, effects like Dragonfly Reverb and CHOW Tape Model, sequencers like Stochas, and many more —
ready to install with one click.

<Shot id="preferences/downloads" alt="Settings ▸ Downloads ▸ Plug-ins with the plug-in list" />

## Finding a plug-in

Filter by **All**, **Instruments**, **Effects** or **Installed** (each with a count) and search by name.
Each row shows the plug-in's type, author, version and licence, a one-line description, its download
size, and icons for the systems it runs on (hover for the processors). Plug-ins that can't be
installed on this computer are listed last with the reason. **Refresh** reloads the registry.

## Installing, updating, removing

- **Install** — downloads the plug-in from its project's own release, checks it against the
  registry's checksum, unpacks it and scans it. Installers are never run.
- **Update to …** — appears when a newer version is in the registry.
- **Remove** — uninstalls it; projects that use it open without it until you install it again.
- **Source** — opens the plug-in's repository.

A progress bar with **Cancel** stays at the bottom of the window. Downloads keep going after you
close Settings — their progress moves to the status line of the main window.
