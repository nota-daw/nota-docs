Nota builds a catalogue of your installed plug-ins by scanning them in a separate process (so a
misbehaving plug-in can't crash Nota).

## Where Nota looks

- **VST3** — the system's standard VST3 folders: on macOS `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3` and
  `/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3`; on Windows `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3`; on Linux `~/.vst3`,
  `/usr/lib/vst3` and `/usr/local/lib/vst3` — plus the folder Nota installs registry plug-ins into, and
  any folder you add.
- **AU** (macOS) — the system's Audio Unit registry.

## Adding a folder and rescanning

<Shot id="preferences/plugins" alt="Settings ▸ Plug-ins: scan folders and Rescan" />

1. Open **Settings ▸ Plug-ins**.
2. **Add folder…** adds a folder to search for VST3 plug-ins; **Remove** takes one away.
3. **Rescan plugins** scans everything again. The status says how many folders it searched and how
   many plug-ins it found.

Plug-ins installed from [Settings ▸ Downloads](../get-plugins/) are scanned automatically.
