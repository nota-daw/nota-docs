Nota MIDI Effect Rack sits with the other MIDI effects, before the track's instrument. Its **chains**
each take part of what you play and run it through their own MIDI effects — an arpeggio above C3,
chords below, accents only on hard hits — and their notes merge into one instrument.

<Shot id="devices/racks/midi-effect-rack/card" alt="Nota MIDI Effect Rack: three chains — Arp lead, Chords, Accents — with the Arp lead chain's Arp and Scale" />

It uses the same card as the [Instrument Rack](../instrument-rack/): [macros](../macros/), the
**Chains**, **Zones** and **Macros** tabs, the **Chain** and **Output** panel. Find it in the Browser
under **MIDI effects · Racks**. A chain's MIDI effects move, copy and paste as in the Instrument
Rack — between chains, racks and the track's own MIDI effects.

## Chains

A chain is a row of built-in MIDI effects (Arp, Chord, Scale, Length, Velocity, Random) that run left
to right. Drop them from the Browser on the chain or on the dashed target; a MIDI effect dropped on
the chain list starts a new chain. A rack holds up to 16 chains.

In the **Chain** panel, **Volume** scales the velocity of the chain's notes (100 % leaves it alone,
0 % silences the chain) and **Transpose** moves them up or down by up to 48 semitones.

## Zones

<Shot id="devices/racks/midi-effect-rack/zones" alt="The Zones tab: key and velocity zones, the selector and the select ranges" />

Each chain takes the notes inside its **key** and **velocity** zones; drag the edges to set them and
hover to see which chains a note would reach. In Select mode the **Selector** and each chain's thin
**select range** under its key zone decide instead which chains get notes — map the selector to a
macro to switch parts from a controller.

## Output

<Shot id="devices/racks/midi-effect-rack/output" alt="Output: Chains run, Merge duplicate notes and Pass through outside zones" />

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Parallel** | A note goes to every chain whose zone it hits |
| **Select** | Only the chains whose select range holds the selector get notes |
| **Merge duplicate notes** | Two chains sending the same pitch play it once |
| **Pass through outside zones** | Notes no chain takes reach the instrument unchanged |

## Tips

- **From the track's MIDI effects:** select them (<Kbd>⇧</Kbd>-click for a run) and press
  <Kbd>⌘ G</Kbd> to fold them into a new MIDI Effect Rack; <Kbd>⌘ ⇧ G</Kbd> takes them out again.
- **Split arp:** an Arp chain from C3 up, a Chord chain below, both into one synth.
- **Accents:** a chain with velocity 110–127 and a Random, so only hard hits wander.
- **Parts on a macro:** Select mode, chains with neighbouring select ranges, the selector on a macro.
