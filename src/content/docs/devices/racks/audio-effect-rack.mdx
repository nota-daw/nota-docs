Nota Audio Effect Rack holds several **chains** of effects inside one device. Process a sound three
ways at once and blend them, build a reusable effect "patch" with a few macros on top, or switch
effects by how loud the input is.

<Shot id="devices/racks/audio-effect-rack/card" alt="Nota Audio Effect Rack: macros, the chain list and the selected chain" />

## Modes

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Parallel** (**Prl**) | Every chain processes the input; their outputs are summed |
| **Series** (**Ser**) | The chains run one after another |
| **Select** (**Sel**) | One chain plays, chosen by the input level |

## Chains

**+ Add chain**, then **+ Device** in it — the menu lists the built-in effects and a **Plug-ins**
submenu. Each chain has a name, its own **gain** and meter, **mute** and **solo**.

To start a rack from an effect already on the track, click it and press <Kbd>⌘ G</Kbd> (or
**Group into rack** in its right-click menu): it moves into a new rack's first chain, settings and
all. <Kbd>⌘ ⇧ G</Kbd> (**Ungroup rack**) puts a one-chain rack's devices back in the track's chain.
An effect with a sidechain key, automation or a CV link stays where it is.

## Rack output

**Dry / wet**, **Gain** and **PDC** (latency compensation between chains), with **Macro map**,
**Fold** (hide the chains) and **Save** (store the rack as a preset). Eight named
[macros](../macros/) control any parameters in the chains.

## Tips

- **Parallel compression in a box:** a dry chain plus a chain with a heavy Compressor, Parallel mode.
- **New York reverb:** a chain with only a Reverb set to Wet only, blended under the dry chain.
