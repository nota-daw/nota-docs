Nota Audio Effect Rack holds several **chains** of effects inside one device. Process a sound
several ways at once and blend them, build a reusable effect "patch" with a few macros on top, or
switch between effect chains with one control.

<Shot id="devices/racks/audio-effect-rack/card" alt="Nota Audio Effect Rack: macros, the chain list, the chosen chain's effects and Rack out" />

It uses the same card as the [Instrument Rack](../instrument-rack/): [macros](../macros/), the
**Chains**, **Select** and **Macros** tabs, the **Chain** and **Rack out** panel. A chain's effects
move, copy and paste as they do there — and between the rack and the track's device chain.

## Chains

**+ Chain** adds one (or drop an effect on the chain list); the dashed target at the end of a chain
adds an effect — the menu lists the built-in effects and a **Plug-ins** submenu. Each chain has a
name, its own **Volume**, **Pan**, level, **mute** and **solo**. Right-click a chain to duplicate or
delete it.

## Chains run

<Shot id="devices/racks/audio-effect-rack/rack-out" alt="Rack out: Chains run, Dry/wet, Gain and the switches" />

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Parallel** | Every chain processes the input; their outputs are summed |
| **Serial** | Chain 1 feeds chain 2, top to bottom |
| **Select** | Only the chains whose select range holds the selector play |

Rack out also holds **Dry/wet**, **Gain** (±12 dB), **Follow input level** and **Latency
compensation** (lines up chains with different latency).

## Select

<Shot id="devices/racks/audio-effect-rack/select" alt="The Select tab: the selector and each chain's select range" />

Each chain has a **select range** from 0 to 127; drag its edges. Drag the **Selector** to choose
which chains play, map it to a macro (right-click the selector, or **Map to the selector** in a
macro's menu) and automate the macro, or switch on **Follow input level** so the input's loudness
moves it. The ranges only count in Select mode.

## Tips

- **Parallel compression in a box:** a dry chain plus a chain with a heavy Compressor, Parallel.
- **One knob, three effects:** three chains with neighbouring select ranges, the selector on a macro.
- **Wrap and unwrap:** click an effect on the track and press <Kbd>⌘ G</Kbd> (or **Group into
  rack** in its right-click menu) to move it into a new rack's first chain, settings and all;
  <Kbd>⌘ ⇧ G</Kbd> (**Ungroup rack**) puts a one-chain rack's devices back. An effect with a
  sidechain key, automation or a CV link stays where it is.
