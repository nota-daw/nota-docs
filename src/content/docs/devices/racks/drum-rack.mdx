Nota Drum Rack plays a different sound on every pad. Load one-shots or instruments onto the pads,
give each its own effects, and program it from the piano roll or the **Pattern** step grid.

<Shot id="devices/racks/drum-rack/card" alt="Nota Drum Rack with the Volta kit: the pad grid and the selected pad" />

## Kits

The **kit picker** in the card header loads any of the **25 factory kits** in place, and names the
kit the rack holds: *Volta*, *Kompakt*, *Micron*, *Linnwood*, *Atelier*, *Cellar*, *Neon*, *Foundry*,
*Terra*, *Aether*, *Brass Room*, *Breakline*, *Bunker*, *Pixel*, *Velvet*, *Circuit*, *Yard*, *Byte*,
*Lagoon*, *Titan*, *Shuffle*, *Mirrorball*, *Pit*, *Hyper* and *Crate* — from warm analog and 80s PCM
to jungle breaks, amapiano log drums and orchestral percussion.

Each kit has 16 pads on the General MIDI map (notes 36–51), hats choked together, its own swing and
humanize, effects on the pads (kick saturation, snare and clap reverb, hat delay) and its own macros.
The sounds are synthesised on first launch, and appear in the browser's **Files** tab under **Nota
Kits**, ready to drag anywhere.

## Pads

A 4 × 4 grid, pad 1 bottom-left as on hardware, in banks **C1**–**C4** (16 notes each, up to 64 pads).

- **Click** a pad to play and select it. A sounding pad lights in its colour.
- **Drop** samples on a pad to load them (several fill the pads after it); an empty pad also offers an
  instrument.
- **Right-click** a pad to rename it, set its **choke** group, open its chain or remove it.
- **Fold** hides the empty pads. **Swing** and **Humanize** shape the kit's feel.

The **Pad** panel on the right edits the selected pad: its one-shot with a playhead, **Volume**,
**Pan**, **Tune**, **Decay**, the **choke** group (pads in one group cut each other — open and closed
hats), and its instrument's editor.

## Mixer, Chain and Macro

<Shot id="devices/racks/drum-rack/mixer" alt="Nota Drum Rack, Mixer view" />

- **Mixer** — the bank's loaded pads as rows: volume, pan, mute, solo, choke.
- **Chain** — the selected pad's device chain: its instrument and its own effects.
- **Macro** — the kit's eight [macros](../macros/), mapped onto pad controls, pad instruments and the
  pads' effects.

<Shot id="devices/racks/drum-rack/macro" alt="Nota Drum Rack, Macro view" />

## Programming

Double-click the clip and use the **Pattern** tab in the detail panel for a step grid with a row per
pad — or the piano roll, where each pad is a note. Audio clips dragged from the Arrangement land on
the next free pad.
