Nota Instrument Rack holds several **chains** on one track — each its own MIDI effects, instrument
and effects. Layer a pad under a pluck, split the keyboard into bass and lead, switch sounds by how
hard you play, or keep an instrument together with its Arp.

<Shot id="devices/racks/instrument-rack/card" alt="Nota Instrument Rack: macros, the chain list, the chosen chain's devices and the Chain panel" />

**Layout:** eight [macros](../macros/) on the left; the **Chains**, **Zones** and **Macros** tabs in the
middle; the **Chain** and **Rack out** panel on the right; a status line along the bottom. The
Audio Effect and MIDI Effect racks use the same card.

## Chains

The list shows every chain: its colour, name, **M** (mute) and **S** (solo), its key and velocity
zone, and its level. Click a chain to choose it; double-click its name to rename it. Right-click it
to **Duplicate** or **Delete** it.

To the right are the chosen chain's devices, left to right as the signal flows: its **MIDI** effects,
its instrument, its audio effects. Each compact card shows the device's key parameters; drag a
slider to change one, **Open** shows the device's full editor. The dashed targets add a device
(click for the list, or drop a device or a preset from the Browser). MIDI effects run in the first
16 chains.

Drag a device by its header to move it within its section — a bar shows where it lands — or drop
it on another chain's row in the list to move it there; its macro mappings go with it. Click a
device to select it, then use <Kbd>⌘ C</Kbd> / <Kbd>⌘ X</Kbd> / <Kbd>⌘ V</Kbd> / <Kbd>⌘ D</Kbd> /
<Kbd>Delete</Kbd> or its right-click menu (**Move left**, **Move right**, **Copy**, **Cut**,
**Paste**, **Duplicate**, **Delete**). The clipboard is the [device chain](../../)'s too: copy an
effect on the track and paste it into a chain, or the other way round. A pasted device brings
its settings but no macro mappings; an effect keyed from another track loses its sidechain in a
rack. A chain's row menu and the dashed targets offer **Paste** at the end of the chain.

**+ Chain** (or an instrument dropped on the list) adds a layer. An instrument dropped on a chain
replaces its instrument.

Every change to the chains undoes with <Kbd>⌘ Z</Kbd>: adding, duplicating or removing a chain, its
devices, its name, its zones, and the macros' mappings.

## Nota Mosaic and a Drum Rack as a chain

A chain can also hold **Nota Mosaic** or a **Nota Drum Rack** — drop it from the Browser on the rack
(a new chain) or on a chain (it replaces the chain's instrument), or pick it in the chain's instrument
menu.

- **Mosaic**: **GUI** opens the full [Mosaic](../../instruments/mosaic/) editor for that chain —
  presets, zones, loading progress. The program is saved with the rack.
- **Drum Rack**: **GUI** opens the [Drum Rack](../drum-rack/) card in a window — pads, the pad editor,
  macros, and the factory kits in the window's preset picker. Give the chain a key zone (say C1–B1)
  and the kit plays there while the other chains play the rest of the keyboard. To turn the Drum
  Rack's macros from outside, map a rack macro to them: they are the chain instrument's parameters.

Racks nest **one level**: a Drum Rack goes in an Instrument Rack chain only, not on a Drum Rack pad or
inside another rack, and an Instrument Rack never goes in a chain. A Drum Rack in a chain has no
**Swing** / **Humanize** — those shift the whole track's notes, so they belong to a Drum Rack track.

## Zones

<Shot id="devices/racks/instrument-rack/zones" alt="The Zones tab: key and velocity zones per chain, with the probe on E4" />

Each chain plays only the notes inside its **key** zone and its **velocity** zone. Drag a zone's edges
to set them; overlapping zones layer, separate zones split. Hover the zones to test a note: a line
marks the key, the chains that would play it light up, and the footer names them.

## Chain panel and Rack out

**Chain** shows the chosen chain's **Volume** and **Pan**, its zones (click to open the Zones tab),
**Duplicate** and **Delete**.

<Shot id="devices/racks/instrument-rack/rack-out" alt="Rack out: Volume, Glide and Mono (last note)" />

**Rack out** holds the rack's **Volume**, **Glide** and **Mono (last note)**. With mono on the rack
plays one note at a time and the last key wins; releasing it returns to the key still held. Glide
slides each new note from the previous pitch on instruments that take per-note pitch bend. Chains
that hold MIDI effects get the notes as played — their effects decide what sounds.

## Tips

- **Bass and lead on one keyboard:** two chains, keys up to B2 and from C3.
- **Velocity layers:** the same zone twice, velocity 0–100 and 101–127.
- **An instrument and its Arp in one preset:** drop Nota Arp into the chain's MIDI section, save the
  rack as a preset.
