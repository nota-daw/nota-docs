Nota Instrument Rack holds several **chains** — each an instrument with its own effects — on one
track. Layer a pad under a pluck, split a keyboard into bass and lead, or switch sounds by how hard
you play.

<Shot id="devices/racks/instrument-rack/card" alt="Nota Instrument Rack: macros, the chain list and the selected chain's devices" />

**Layout:** eight **macros** on the left, the **chain** list in the middle, the selected chain's
devices on the right.

## Chains

**+ Add chain** adds one; pick its instrument, then add effects with **+ Device**. Each chain has a
name, its own **gain** and meter, **mute** and **solo**.

**Zones** decide which chain plays: a **key** zone (a range of notes) and a **velocity** zone (a range
of how hard). Overlapping zones layer; separate zones split. The zone map shows them across the
keyboard.

## Devices inside a chain

Each device shows as a card with its knobs. **GUI** opens the device's full editor (for a plug-in, its
own window; for the Sampler, its tabbed editor) in a [device window](../../../views/detail-panel/#device-windows);
**Params** lists its parameters. Drag cards to reorder; ✕ removes one.

## Rack output and macros

**Volume** and **Glide** for the whole rack. Eight named [macros](../macros/) turn many parameters in
the chains at once. **Fold** hides the chains; **Save** stores the whole rack as a preset.
