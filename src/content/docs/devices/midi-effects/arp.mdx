Nota Arp turns held notes into a running pattern. Play a chord, get a sequence — up, down, in the
order you played, random — shaped step by step by its groove lanes.

<Shot id="devices/midi-effects/arp/card" alt="Nota Arp in its large view, Pattern window" />

MIDI effects sit before the instrument in a track's chain: add one from the browser's **MIDI
effects** tab. The **S / L** switch in the header picks a compact or a full card.

## Order

**Up**, **Down**, **Up · Down**, **Down · Up**, **Converge**, **Diverge**, **As played**, **Random** or
**Chord** (the whole chord on each step). The order walks the whole range over the chosen octaves —
Up · Down over two octaves climbs C3 … B4 and back.

## Rate and Play

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Free** / **Sync** | Rate in milliseconds, or a note division |
| **Gate** | Note length as a share of the step |
| **Swing** | Delays the off-steps |
| **Vel Amt** | How much the played velocity carries through |
| **Octaves** | How many octaves the pattern spans |
| **Retrig** | Restart the pattern: **Off**, on each new **Note**, or on the **Beat** |
| **Transpose** | Shift the output |
| **Steps** | Loop length of the pattern |
| **Hold** | Keep playing after you let go |
| **Restart** | Start the pattern over now |

## Groove

<Shot id="devices/midi-effects/arp/groove" alt="Nota Arp, Groove lanes" />

Six 16-step lanes — **Velocity**, **Length**, **Chance**, **Ratchet** (repeats a step's note),
**Transpose** and **On** — draw per-step variation into the pattern. The window animates: the head
walks the steps and the cell that's sounding lights up.

## Presets

30 factory presets, from simple runs (**Up 1/16**, **Down 1/8**, **Octave Up-Down**, **Down-Up
Cascade**, **Converge**, **Diverge Bloom**, **As Played Hook**, **Four-Octave Rise**) to grooves
(**Trance Gate**, **House Offbeat Stab**, **Swing Groove**, **Velocity Accents**, **Bar Retrig Riff**,
**Triplet Roll**, **Free Run 150 ms**) and beyond.
