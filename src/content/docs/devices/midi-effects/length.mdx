Nota Length decides how long notes last, whatever was played: tight staccato, even eighths, long
drones from short taps — or notes that start when you **release** a key.

<Shot id="devices/midi-effects/length/card" alt="Nota Length: length mode, the gate window and the modifiers" />

## Length

| Mode | The note lasts |
|---|---|
| **Sync** | A tempo division (eight of them) |
| **ms** | A fixed time |
| **Gate %** | A share of how long the key was held — it can shorten or stretch |

**Start from** — **Note-on** (normal) or **Note-off**: the note fires when the key is released.

## Modifiers

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Vel → Len** | Bipolar: louder notes longer (+) or shorter (−) |
| **Key → Len** | Bipolar: lower notes longer (+) or shorter (−) |
| **Random** | ± spread per note |
| **Legato** | Stretches each note to the start of the next |
| **Clip length limit** | A note never crosses the bar line it started in |

The **Gate** window shows a bar of demo notes — input outlined, output filled, Random's range as a
band.

## Presets

32 factory presets: divisions (**Staccato**, **Eighths**, **Tenuto 1/4**, **Half Notes**, **Whole
Bar**, **Dotted Eighth**, **Quarter Triplet**, **32nd Blips**), fixed times (**Blip 40ms**, **Fixed
120ms**, **Fixed 250ms**, **Fixed 500ms**, **One Second**), gates (**Half Gate**, **Tight Quarter
Gate**, **Almost Legato**, **Stretch 150%**, …) and more.
