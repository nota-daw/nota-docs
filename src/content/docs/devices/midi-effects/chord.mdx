Nota Chord plays a chord from every note you play: one finger, full harmony.

<Shot id="devices/midi-effects/chord/card" alt="Nota Chord: type, shifts and the result keyboard" />

## Type and shifts

**Type** picks a shape — **Maj7**, **Min7**, **Sus4**, **5th** — or **Custom**. The **Shifts** are six
added voices: each has an on / off dot, an interval of −12 … +12 semitones (named — M3, P5, M7…) and
a velocity offset you drag. Editing a shift turns the type to Custom. **Keep root** plays the note
you pressed too.

## Result

The chord's note names and a three-octave keyboard — your note, the added ones, an unplayed root
outlined. It strums once per bar while the transport runs, and after each edit, so you hear the
shape.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Strum** | Spreads the chord's notes low to high, so many ms apart — a guitar strum |
| **Spread** | Opens the voicing: at ⅓ every other voice goes up an octave, at ⅔ the rest too |
| **Fold in scale** | An added note outside the scale (**Fold Key**, major or minor) drops a semitone into it |

## Presets

34 factory presets: triads and sevenths (**Major Triad**, **Minor Triad**, **Sus2**, **Sus4**, **Power
Chord**, **Augmented**, **Maj7**, **Min7**, **Dominant 7**, **Sixth**, **Minor 6**, **Diminished 7**,
**Half-Diminished**) and wide and modern voicings (**Maj7 Wide**, **Add9 Open**, **Min9 Open**,
**Neo-Soul m11**, **Rootless Maj9**, **Rootless Dom9**, **Quartal**, …).
