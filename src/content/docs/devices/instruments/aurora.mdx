Nota Aurora is a wavetable synth: each oscillator reads through a **table** of waveforms, and
moving its **position** morphs the sound from one shape to the next. It's built for pads that
evolve, digital basses, plucks and bells.

<Shot id="devices/instruments/aurora/card" alt="Nota Aurora, Osc tab with the wavetable stack" />

**Signal flow:** Osc 1 + Osc 2 + Sub (with unison) → Filter 1 and Filter 2 (in parallel or in
series, each source routed to F1, F2, both or dry) → amp envelope → drive → chorus → reverb.

The **wheels** on the left (pitch bend, mod wheel) are on every tab. The rail on the right shows
**Global** controls or the **Macros**.

## Osc

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Table** | The wavetable bank: **Analog**, **Pulse**, **Formant** or **Chroma** |
| **Position** | Where in the table the oscillator reads (16 frames) |
| **Warp** · warp mode | Bends the wave: **Off**, **Sync**, **Bend**, **PWM** or **Fold** |
| **Level** · **Oct** · **Semi** · **Fine** | Level and tuning of each oscillator; each can be switched off |
| **Sub** | A sub oscillator: level, octave and wave |
| **Unison** | Voices, **Detune** and **Spread** of the unison stack |

## Filter

<Shot id="devices/instruments/aurora/filter" alt="Nota Aurora, Filter tab" />

Two filters, **F1** and **F2**: **LP**, **HP**, **BP** or **Notch**, with slope, cutoff, resonance
and **Env**, **LFO** and **Key** amounts — drag the response for cutoff and resonance.
**Routing** sends Osc 1, Osc 2 and Sub each to F1, F2, both or dry, with the filters in
**Parallel** or **Series**.

## Env and LFO

**Env 1** shapes the amplitude, **Env 2** is free for the mod matrix; both are drawn and
draggable. Two **LFOs** — shape (including S&H), rate, depth, optional tempo sync — each show
where they're routed, over a window that draws the selected LFO.

## Mod

<Shot id="devices/instruments/aurora/mod" alt="Nota Aurora, Mod tab with the modulation matrix" />

An **8 × 7 matrix**: sources (Env 1, Env 2, LFO 1, LFO 2, Velocity, Key, Mod Wheel, Random) down
the side, destinations (Pitch, Osc 2 Pitch, Position, Cutoff, Reso, Level, Pan) across. Drag a
cell up for a positive amount, down for a negative one. **Clear all** resets it.

**Macros** — eight of them, three to a page in the rail — each set a value, an amount and one of
twelve targets. Map them to a controller with MIDI Learn for hands-on morphing.

## FX

<Shot id="devices/instruments/aurora/fx" alt="Nota Aurora, FX tab" />

Each block has its own switch:

- **Drive** — **Tube**, **Tape** or **Fold**, with **Tone**.
- **Chorus** — 1, 2 or 4 voices, with rate.
- **Reverb** — **Room**, **Hall** or **Plate**, with **Size**.

## Global

Voice mode (**Poly 16** or **Mono**), bend range, position, cutoff, unison, gain, pan and the
track's meter.

## Presets

25 factory presets: pads (**Warm Pad**, **Glacier Pad**, **Glass Choir**, **Formant Pad**, **Air
Strings**), basses (**Sub Bass**, **Reese Bass**, **Acid Table**, **Wobble Wave**, **Rubber
Bass**), leads (**Detuned Lead**, **Super Saw**, **Fold Lead**, **Hollow Lead**), keys and plucks
(**Shimmer Arp**, **Motion Keys**, **Bright Pluck**, **Nylon Keys**, **Tape Keys**), bells
(**Glass Bells**, **Digital Bell**, **Chime Sequence**) and motion (**Deep Drone**, **Aurora
Sweep**, **Split Filter**).

## Tips

- **Make it move:** route LFO 1 to **Position** in the matrix with a slow rate — the timbre
  drifts through the table.
- **Mod wheel as a morph:** put the Mod Wheel on Position and Cutoff together.
