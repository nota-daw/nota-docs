Nota Bass is tuned for low end: one oscillator that morphs from sine to pulse, a sub oscillator
underneath, a filter with drive, and a mono voice with glide. Subs, 808s, acid lines, reese and
wobble basses.

<Shot id="devices/instruments/bass/card" alt="Nota Bass, Signal tab" />

**Signal flow:** Osc + Sub → pre-drive → filter (with its envelope, key and LFO) → amp envelope →
output drive.

## Signal

| Section | Controls |
|---|---|
| **Osc** | **Shape** morphs sine → triangle → saw → pulse; **PW** at the pulse end; **Oct**, **Semi**, **Level** |
| **Sub** | Sine, triangle or square, 1 or 2 octaves down, **Level** |
| **Filter** | **LP**, **HP**, **BP** or **Notch**, 12 or 24 dB; **Cutoff**, **Reso**, **Drive**, **Env** and **Key** amounts — drag the response pad |

## Mod

<Shot id="devices/instruments/bass/mod" alt="Nota Bass, Mod tab with the envelopes and the LFO" />

The **amp** and **filter** envelopes, drawn and draggable; the **LFO** (rate, wave including S&H)
with two destinations — the filter and **LFO → Pitch**; and the velocity amounts **Vel → Amp** and
**Vel → Fil**.

## Global

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mono** / **Poly 16** | Mono plays the last note you hold — a proper bass |
| **Legato** | In Mono, a note played over a held one slides into it without a new attack |
| **Glide** | Slide time between notes |
| **Unison** | Thicker, detuned voices |
| **Drive** | Output saturation |
| **Bend** · **Volume** · **Pan** | Bend range (1–12 semitones), level, position |

The **mod wheel** opens the LFO onto the cutoff — a hand-played wobble.

## Presets

25 factory presets: subs (**Sub Rumble**, **808 Sub**, **Dub Sub**, **Techno Rumble**, **Soft
Round**), classic mono (**Pluck Bass**, **Classic Mono**, **Pulse Bass**, **Rubber Bass**, **Stab
Bass**, **Octave Bass**, **Drift Bass**), acid (**Acid 303**, **Acid Slide**), heavy (**Reese
Bass**, **Growl Bass**, **Wobble Bass**, **Hoover Bass**, **Fuzz Bass**, **Vowel Bass**, **Wheel
Wobble**, **S&H Bass**) and played (**Finger Bass**, **Picked Bass**, **Funk Slap**).

## Tips

- **303-style slides:** Mono + **Legato** + a little **Glide**; overlap the notes in the piano
  roll where you want a slide.
- **Keep the sub clean:** let the Sub carry the lows and push **Drive** on the Osc for the
  upper harmonics.
