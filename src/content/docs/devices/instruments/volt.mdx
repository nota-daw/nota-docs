Nota Volt is a virtual-analog synth modelled on classic two-oscillator analogs, with a twist:
two full **signal paths**. Each source can go to Filter 1, Filter 2 or both, and each filter has
its own amp with pan — so one patch can be two layered sounds.

<Shot id="devices/instruments/volt/card" alt="Nota Volt, Osc tab" />

**Signal flow:**

```
Osc 1 ┐                          ┌─ Filter 1 → Amp 1 (pan, level) ─┐
Osc 2 ┼─ route each to F1 / F2 ──┤                                 ├─→ out
Noise ┘                          └─ Filter 2 → Amp 2 (pan, level) ─┘
               (Filter 1 can also feed Filter 2: "To F2")
```

The **wheels** on the left (pitch bend, mod wheel) stay on every tab. The rail on the right shows
**Global** or **Voice** controls.

## Osc

Osc 1, Osc 2 and Noise as a table: **wave**, **Oct**, **Semi**, **Fine**, **Shape**, **PW**,
start **Phase**, **Level** and **Route** (which filter it goes to). Noise has a **Color**.

- **Shape** morphs the chosen wave toward the next one in the order sine → triangle → saw →
  square. At 0 (off) you hear the wave as picked; halfway from saw you hear half saw, half
  square. On the square it does nothing, as there is no wave after it.
- **PW** (pulse width) narrows or widens the square from 5 % to 95 %. At 50 % it is a plain
  square; a narrow pulse sounds thinner and more nasal.

## Filter

<Shot id="devices/instruments/volt/filter" alt="Nota Volt, Filter tab" />

Two state-variable filters — **LP**, **HP**, **BP** or **Notch**, 12 or 24 dB — each with
cutoff, resonance, and **Env**, **Key** and **LFO** amounts. Drag the response pad for cutoff and
resonance. On Filter 1, **To F2** sends part of it into Filter 2 for serial filtering.

Two knobs act on both filters, so they stay put when you switch between 1 and 2:

- **Drive** saturates the signal on its way into the filters, from clean up to 12 times the
  gain. It adds grit and makes the resonance growl, as on an acid bass.
- **Wheel** (Wheel → LFO) lets the mod wheel open each filter's LFO onto its cutoff, up to ±2
  octaves on top of the filter's own LFO amount. Raise the wheel to bring in a wobble.

## Env

<Shot id="devices/instruments/volt/env" alt="Nota Volt, Env tab with the amp and filter envelopes" />

The **amp** and **filter** envelopes side by side (A · D · S · R), drawn and draggable.

## LFO

**LFO 1**, **LFO 2** and **Vibrato** as a table: shape (sine, triangle, square, S&H), rate, depth,
tempo sync and **Fade** (a per-note fade-in), each showing where it lands. Vibrato can be handed
to the mod wheel (**By wheel**).

## Mod

<Shot id="devices/instruments/volt/mod" alt="Nota Volt, Mod tab with the modulation matrix" />

A **7 × 6 matrix**: Amp Env, Flt Env, LFO 1, LFO 2, Velocity, Key and Mod Wheel against Pitch, Osc 2
Pitch, Cutoff, Reso, Level and Pan. Drag a cell up for +, down for −.

## Macro

Eight macros, each a value, an amount and one of twelve targets — the six matrix destinations,
the three source levels, Filter 2's cutoff and either LFO's rate.

## Rail

**Global:** voice mode, bend range (1–12 semitones), cutoff, resonance, glide, **Gain**,
**Drive** and **Pan**.

- **Poly** plays up to 16 notes. **Mono** plays one: each new note starts its envelopes again,
  and when you let go of the top key the note falls back to a key you are still holding.
  **Legato** is Mono where a note played over a held one slides in without a new attack.
- **Drive** saturates the output, from clean up to 8 times the gain, and keeps the level in
  check as it pushes.

**Voice:** both output amps (level and pan), the unison section, and **Vel Amp** / **Vel Fil**
(how much velocity changes the level and the cutoff).

- **Unison** picks the voices per oscillator. **1** adds one detuned partner to each
  oscillator, and **Detune** sets how far (up to 25 cents). **2**, **4** and **7** stack that
  many copies of each oscillator, spread across ±50 cents by **Detune**, and **Spread** pans
  them across the stereo field: the supersaw sound. With 2 to 7 voices and some Spread, each
  side of the stereo field gets its own filter.

## Presets

74 factory presets. Volt's own 25: pads (**Warm Pad**, **String Machine**, **Choir Pad**, **Glass Pad**), basses
(**Fat Bass**, **Sub Sine**, **Wobble Bass**, **Acid Bass**, **Reese Bass**, **Growl Bass**), leads
(**Bright Lead**, **Super Saw**, **Solo Lead**, **Hard Lead**), keys (**Soft Pluck**, **Drifting
Keys**, **Electric Keys**, **Bell Tone**, **Clav Stab**) and two-path or motion patches (**Split
Filter**, **Dark Drone**, **Sample & Hold**, **Noise Sweep**, **Siren**, **Serial Filters**).

The other 49 are the former [Nota Synth](../synth/) and [Nota Bass](../bass/) libraries, rebuilt
on Volt so they sound the same. Synth's are filed with Volt's own (Pads, Bass, Leads, Keys &
Plucks, Bells & Mallets, Textures & FX). Bass's keep their own folders: **Sub Bass**, **Analog
Bass**, **Acid**, **Growl & Wobble** and **Electric Bass**. Where Volt already had a preset of
the same name, the ported one was renamed: for example Synth's Super Saw is **Super Saw 7** and
Bass's Reese Bass is **Deep Reese**.

## Tips

- **Two sounds in one:** send Osc 1 to F1 (low-pass, panned left) and Osc 2 to F2 (band-pass,
  panned right).
- **Classic S&H bleeps:** LFO 1 on S&H, routed to cutoff, synced to 1/16.
- **Supersaw:** saw on both oscillators, **Unison 7**, Detune around 30 %, Spread up.
- **Sub under a bass:** Osc 2 on sine, Oct −1, routed to the same filter.
- **303 line:** saw or square into a 24 dB low-pass, high resonance, filter envelope up, some
  Drive, **Legato** with a little glide.
