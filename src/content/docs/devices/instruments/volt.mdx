Nota Volt is a virtual-analog synth modelled on classic two-oscillator analogs, with a twist:
two full **signal paths**. Each source can go to Filter 1, Filter 2 or both, and each filter has
its own amp with pan — so one patch can be two layered sounds.

<Shot id="devices/instruments/volt/card" alt="Nota Volt, Osc tab" />

**Signal flow:**

```
Osc 1 ┐                          ┌─ Filter 1 → Amp 1 (pan, level) ─┐
Osc 2 ┼─ route each to F1 / F2 ──┤                                 ├─→ out
Noise ┘                          └─ Filter 2 → Amp 2 (pan, level) ─┘
               (Filter 1 can also feed Filter 2: "To F2")
```

The **wheels** on the left (pitch bend, mod wheel) stay on every tab. The rail on the right shows
**Global** or **Voice** controls.

## Osc

Osc 1, Osc 2 and Noise as a table: **wave**, **Oct**, **Semi**, **Fine**, start **Phase**,
**Level** and **Route** (which filter it goes to). Noise has a **Color**.

## Filter

<Shot id="devices/instruments/volt/filter" alt="Nota Volt, Filter tab" />

Two state-variable filters — **LP**, **HP**, **BP** or **Notch**, 12 or 24 dB — each with
cutoff, resonance, and **Env**, **Key** and **LFO** amounts. Drag the response pad for cutoff and
resonance. On Filter 1, **To F2** sends part of it into Filter 2 for serial filtering.

## Env

<Shot id="devices/instruments/volt/env" alt="Nota Volt, Env tab with the amp and filter envelopes" />

The **amp** and **filter** envelopes side by side (A · D · S · R), drawn and draggable.

## LFO

**LFO 1**, **LFO 2** and **Vibrato** as a table: shape (sine, triangle, square, S&H), rate, depth,
tempo sync and **Fade** (a per-note fade-in), each showing where it lands. Vibrato can be handed
to the mod wheel (**By wheel**).

## Mod

<Shot id="devices/instruments/volt/mod" alt="Nota Volt, Mod tab with the modulation matrix" />

A **7 × 6 matrix**: Amp Env, Flt Env, LFO 1, LFO 2, Velocity, Key and Mod Wheel against Pitch, Osc 2
Pitch, Cutoff, Reso, Level and Pan. Drag a cell up for +, down for −.

## Macro

Eight macros, each a value, an amount and one of twelve targets — the six matrix destinations,
the three source levels, Filter 2's cutoff and either LFO's rate.

## Rail

**Global:** voice mode (**Poly 16** / **Mono**), bend range (1–12 semitones), cutoff, resonance,
glide, gain, pan. **Voice:** both output amps, **Unison** and **Vel → Amp** / **Vel → Filter**.

## Presets

25 factory presets: pads (**Warm Pad**, **String Machine**, **Choir Pad**, **Glass Pad**), basses
(**Fat Bass**, **Sub Sine**, **Wobble Bass**, **Acid Bass**, **Reese Bass**, **Growl Bass**), leads
(**Bright Lead**, **Super Saw**, **Solo Lead**, **Hard Lead**), keys (**Soft Pluck**, **Drifting
Keys**, **Electric Keys**, **Bell Tone**, **Clav Stab**) and two-path or motion patches (**Split
Filter**, **Dark Drone**, **Sample & Hold**, **Noise Sweep**, **Siren**, **Serial Filters**).

## Tips

- **Two sounds in one:** send Osc 1 to F1 (low-pass, panned left) and Osc 2 to F2 (band-pass,
  panned right).
- **Classic S&H bleeps:** LFO 1 on S&H, routed to cutoff, synced to 1/16.
