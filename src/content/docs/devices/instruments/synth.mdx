Nota Synth is the simplest instrument in Nota: one oscillator, an envelope and a filter. It is
the instrument a new track gets by default, and the quickest way to a bass, a pad or a lead
that sits in the mix without a fight.

<Shot id="devices/instruments/synth/card" alt="Nota Synth in its large view, Osc tab" />

**Signal flow:** oscillator (with unison) → amp envelope → filter → pan and gain. Up to 16
voices.

## Two sizes

The **S / L** switch in the header flips the card. **S** shows just the filter curve and four
knobs — Cutoff, Reso, Attack, Release — for quick tweaks; **L** has three tabs (**Osc**,
**Env**, **Filter**) and a rail with the voice and output controls. Both sizes edit the same
sound; a new Synth opens small.

## Osc

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Wave** | Saw, Square, Triangle or Sine |
| **Pulse W** | Pulse width of the square wave |
| **Detune** | Spread of the unison voices against each other |
| **Octave** | −2 … +2 octaves |
| **Unison** | 1, 2, 4 or 7 stacked voices per note, detuned for width |

## Env

<Shot id="devices/instruments/synth/env" alt="Nota Synth, Env tab" />

The amp envelope: **Attack**, **Decay**, **Sustain**, **Release** — drag the breakpoints on the
graph or turn the knobs. **Vel → Vol** sets how much playing harder makes the note louder.

## Filter

<Shot id="devices/instruments/synth/filter" alt="Nota Synth, Filter tab" />

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Off, LP (low-pass), HP (high-pass) or BP (band-pass) |
| **Cutoff** · **Reso** | Where the filter cuts and how much it rings there — or drag the curve |
| **Env → Cutoff** | How far the envelope opens the filter on each note |

## The rail

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Voices** | **Poly 16**, **Mono**, or **Legato** (mono, and a note played over a held one slides without retriggering) |
| **Glide** | Time to slide from one note to the next |
| **Spread** | Stereo width of the unison voices |
| **Volume** · **Pan** | Output level and position |

The strip at the bottom reads the patch back in words — "Saw · unison 4 · detune 12 c".

## Presets

25 factory presets, from **Init Saw** to pads (**Warm Pad**, **Glass Pad**, **String
Ensemble**, **Choir Pad**), basses (**Rubber Bass**, **Sub Bass**, **Acid Bass**, **Growl
Bass**), leads (**Super Saw**, **Solo Lead**, **Slide Lead**), keys (**Electric Piano**,
**Organ Tone**, **Music Box**) and motion (**Drone**, **Air Sweep**, **Filter Sweep**).

## Tips

- **A quick sub bass:** Sine, Mono, a short Attack and Release, Octave −1.
- **Wide pad from one oscillator:** Saw, Unison 7, Detune up, Spread up, a slow Attack and
  Release, LP filter around the middle.
