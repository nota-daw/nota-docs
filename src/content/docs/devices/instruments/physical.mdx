Nota Physical builds sounds the way objects make them: something **strikes** (a mallet, a burst
of noise) and something **rings** (a resonator tuned like a beam, a marimba bar, a string, a
drum skin, a plate or a pipe). It's the instrument for mallets, bells, plucks and struck
percussion that respond to how hard you play.

<Shot id="devices/instruments/physical/card" alt="Nota Physical, Exciter tab" />

**Signal flow:** mallet + noise → resonator 1 and resonator 2 (in series or in parallel) →
output. Up to 8 voices.

## Exciter

What strikes the resonators.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mallet** — level · **Stiff** · **Noise** · **Color** | A strike: harder mallets excite more high partials; noise adds the click of the hit |
| **Noise** — type · **Freq** · **Reso** · level | A filtered noise burst, for breath, bow or scrape |
| **Noise Env** — A · D · S · R | How the noise burst evolves; **Env** sweeps its filter |

## Resonator

<Shot id="devices/instruments/physical/resonator" alt="Nota Physical, Resonator tab with the partials of the bank" />

Two banks of tuned resonators; bank 2 can be switched off. The graph shows each bank's partials
as the engine tunes them — drag ↔ for **Ratio**, ↕ for **Bright**.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Type** | The material: **Beam**, **Marimba**, **String**, **Membrane**, **Plate**, **Pipe** |
| **Decay** | How long it rings |
| **Material** | How fast high partials die compared with low ones — wood to metal |
| **Bright** | How much of the high partials you hear |
| **Inharm** | Bends the partials away from a clean harmonic series — bell-like |
| **Ratio** | Tuning of the partials relative to the note |
| **Hit** | Where it's struck — the centre or the edge |
| **Tune** | Tuning of the bank |
| **Struct** | **1 → 2** (series: bank 1 rings bank 2, like a string on a body) or **1 + 2** (parallel: both struck) |
| **Mix 1+2** | Balance between bank 1 and bank 2 |

## Output

**Poly** (8 voices) or **Mono** (a new strike chokes the last), **Tune**, **Fine**, **Note
off** (how the sound reacts when you release a key), **Volume** and **Pan**.

## Presets

32 factory presets: mallets (**Marimba**, **Vibraphone**, **Xylophone**, **Glockenspiel**,
**Bass Marimba**, **Balafon**), bells (**Glass Bell**, **Tubular**, **Church Bell**, **Music
Box**, **Crystal Chime**, **Gamelan**, **Singing Bowl**, **Wind Chimes**), percussion (**Wood
Block**, **Hand Drum**, **Steel Drum**, **Log Drum**, **Cowbell**, **Clave**, **Sub Thump**),
plucks (**Kalimba**, **Harp Pluck**, **Koto**, **Tine Keys**) and blown or bowed sounds (**Pan
Flute**, **Blown Bottle**, **Bowed Glass**, **Metal Plate**).

## Tips

- **Velocity matters:** a harder strike brings out more partials, so play it from a keyboard or
  shape velocities in the piano roll.
- **From wood to metal:** start from Marimba, raise **Material** and **Inharm** step by step.
