Nota Mosaic plays many samples across the keyboard — a real piano recorded note by note, soft
and hard, with the damper's thump on release. Each sample is a **zone**: a rectangle of keys ×
velocities with its own root note, tuning, gain, pan, start / end and loop. Zones gather in
**groups** — layers with a shared gain and tuning and a **round-robin**, so a repeated note
doesn't sound like a machine gun.

<Shot id="devices/instruments/mosaic/card" alt="Nota Mosaic, Zones tab: the zone map over the keyboard and the Voices rail" />

On a note every zone that covers the key, the velocity and the group's current round-robin step
sounds at once; crossfades blend neighbouring layers. **Release** zones sound when you let go of
the key (or the pedal) — quieter the longer you held it.

## Where the sound comes from

- **Factory presets** — 11 presets on built-in multisamples: **Keys** (**Felt Piano**, **Felt
  Piano Soft**, **Felt Piano Bright**, **Music Box**), **Mallets & Plucks** (**Glass Mallets**,
  **Nylon Guitar**, **Brass Pluck**, **Brass Lead**) and **Pads** (**Warm Pad**, **Warm Pad
  Filtered**). Their samples are made the first time you load one.
- **Sample packs** — every pack installed in **Settings → Downloads → Sample Packs** shows up
  in the preset picker under **Packs**, one preset per instrument it holds. A pack with SFZ
  programs plays them as made, CC articulations included.
- **Your own samples** — right-click a folder in the browser's **Files** tab → **Create
  multisample…**, or drop audio files or a folder on the card (see below).

Samples are referenced, not copied into the project. They load into memory in the background —
the card shows a progress bar and notes stay silent until everything is in. If a project or
preset opens on a computer without its pack, the card says so and offers **Install pack** or
**Locate folder…**.

## Create multisample

Mosaic reads the note, the velocity layer, the round-robin step and the release mark from the
file names (`trombone_gb4.wav`, `_f_`, `_rr2`, `_rel`). On the left are the instruments it found,
each with how sure it is; on the right, the chosen one's zone map with what needs a look — gaps
where neighbours were stretched, duplicates, notes it had to guess by listening. Whether C4 or C3
is middle C differs between libraries, so Mosaic checks the octave by ear and explains its
verdict. **Create N presets** saves one preset per checked instrument under **Packs**, in a folder named after yours.

## Zones

The map is keys across, velocity up, with the keyboard under it; a brass tick marks each zone's
root. Click a zone to select it, drag it to move it, drag an edge to resize it; play the keyboard
to hear it. **Attack / Release** switches between the zones heard on key-down and the release
zones. The row below edits the selected zone: root, layer and round-robin, keys, velocity,
**Fine**, **Gain**, **Pan**. Every edit is one undo step.

## Other tabs

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Sample** | The selected zone's sample: start, end and loop (drag them), loop mode, **Crossfade**, **Gain**, **Snap to zero**, **Trim silence** |
| **Groups** | The layers: zones, gain, tune, round-robin with **RR mode** (**Sequential**, **Random**, **No repeat**), exclusive groups; **Release Triggers** with their level and the **Length → level** curve |
| **Pitch** | **Transpose**, **Detune**, **Keytrack**, the zone's root and fine tune, the MPE **Bend** range |
| **Env** | The amp envelope — drag it or use **Attack**, **Decay**, **Sustain**, **Release**; **Vel curve**; MPE **Pressure** |
| **Filter** | The filter response (drag it): type, **Cutoff**, **Reso**, **Keytrack**, **Env → Cutoff**; MPE **Slide** |

## Voices

The rail on the right: **Poly** with 16, 32, 64 or 128 voices, **Mono** (legato, with glide)
or **Choke**; **Volume**, **Pan**, **Glide**, the **Pedal**, **Output**, **Vel → Vol** and the meter. Click **Poly** for
its popover: what the voices are doing, the stealing order (released voices first, then the
quietest, then the oldest), **Per key** — how many strikes of one key may ring — and **Old voice
fade**.

The S size shows the name, a mini map, the zone and layer count and the memory it uses.

## Playing it

- **Sustain pedal:** your keyboard's pedal (CC64) holds notes; release zones sound when it lifts.
- **MPE:** bend retunes the note, pressure adds level, slide opens the filter — see
  [MIDI controllers](../../../control/midi/).

## Tips

- **A drier piano:** lower **Rel vol** on the release triggers in **Groups**.
- **A pad from a pluck:** a slow **Attack** and a long **Release** in **Env**, then close the
  filter a little.
- **Your own kit of notes:** name files like `bass_c2_f_rr1.wav` and **Create multisample** lays
  them out with no manual mapping.
