Nota Operator is an FM synth: four oscillators — **operators** A, B, C, D — bend each other's
phase to make harmonics a filter can't. Electric pianos, bells, glassy pads, slap and FM basses.

<Shot id="devices/instruments/operator/card" alt="Nota Operator, Operators tab" />

**Signal flow:** operators A–D, wired by the **algorithm** (modulators bend their targets; A can
feed back into itself) → carriers → filter → volume.

In the card, **brass** marks a carrier — what reaches the output; **teal** a modulator. The
**wheels** (pitch bend, mod wheel on the FM depth) stay on every tab.

## Operators

Each operator, as a row: its role in words, **Wave** (Sin, Tri, Saw, Sqr), **Crs** (frequency ratio:
0.5, 1, 2 … 14 or 16), **Fine** (detune), **Level** with its dB.

## Algorithm

<Shot id="devices/instruments/operator/algorithm" alt="Nota Operator, Algorithm tab with the topologies and the routing diagram" />

Eleven topologies as sketches — chains, stacks, parallel (additive) — and a routing diagram:
drag one operator onto another to re-route. **Feedback** feeds operator A back into itself for
grit and noise.

## Filter · Amp

<Shot id="devices/instruments/operator/filter-amp" alt="Nota Operator, Filter · Amp tab with the response and the live spectrum" />

A subtractive filter after the FM: **LP**, **HP** or **BP**, **Freq**, **Reso**, **Key Trk**, and
the output volume — beside a live harmonic **spectrum**.

## Rail

**Global:** **FM** depth, **Feedback**, **Tone**, **Glide**, bend range, voice mode (**Poly 12** /
**Mono**), **Volume**, and **Vel → FM** (play harder for a brighter note). **Env:** the selected
operator's ADSR, drawn and draggable, with **Key → Lvl** and **Vel → Lvl** tracking.

## Presets

25 factory presets: pads (**Glass Pad**, **Air Pad**, **Choir Pad**, **Warm Strings**), basses
(**FM Bass**, **Slap Bass**, **Sub Sine**, **Growl Bass**), leads (**Bright Lead**, **Reed Lead**,
**Metal Lead**, **Whistle**), keys (**E-Piano**, **Tine Keys**, **Wurly**, **Clav**,
**Harpsichord**, **Drawbar Organ**), bells and mallets (**FM Bell**, **Tubular Bell**, **Music
Box**, **Marimba**, **Kalimba**, **Log Drum**) and **Brass Section**.

## Tips

- **Classic e-piano:** a 1:1 carrier with a modulator at a ratio of 14 and a short decay on the
  modulator — that's the "tine".
- **Brighter with velocity:** raise **Vel → FM** so soft notes stay mellow.
