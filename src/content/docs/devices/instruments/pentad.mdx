Nota Pentad is a polyphonic analog synth in the spirit of the Sequential Prophet-5 (Rev 3). Each
voice has its own drift, so chords breathe like the real thing — brassy stabs, sync leads, poly-mod
bells and warm strings.

<Shot id="devices/instruments/pentad/card" alt="Nota Pentad, Oscillators tab" />

**Signal flow per voice:** Osc A (saw + pulse, hard-sync to B) + Osc B (saw, triangle, pulse; can
run as a low-frequency LFO) + noise → mixer → 4-pole low-pass → VCA. **Poly-Mod** lets the filter
envelope and Osc B modulate Osc A's frequency and pulse width and the cutoff; **Wheel-Mod** sends
the LFO and noise through the mod wheel.

## Oscillators

Osc A and Osc B: **Oct**, **Fine**, wave shapes, pulse width, **Sync** (A to B), **Lo freq** on B
(turn it into an LFO) and **Kbd** (B follows the keyboard or not). **Glide** (Off, On, Leg) and the
**Mode** (**Poly**, **Uni**, **Mono**) live here too.

## Filter · Amp

<Shot id="devices/instruments/pentad/filter-amp" alt="Nota Pentad, Filter · Amp tab" />

The 4-pole filter — **Cutoff**, resonance, **Keytrack** (0, ½, 1) and envelope amount — with its
**Filter env** and the **Amp env** (A · D · S · R each). The resonance self-oscillates as a clean,
playable sine; bass thins as resonance rises, as on the original — **Bass comp** restores it.

## Poly Mod

<Shot id="devices/instruments/pentad/poly-mod" alt="Nota Pentad, Poly Mod tab" />

**Poly-Mod** — the filter envelope and Osc B, at audio rate, into Osc A's frequency, Osc A's pulse
width and the filter: metallic bells and FM-like growl. **Wheel-Mod** — the LFO (with Free / Sync)
mixed with noise, its amount plus the mod wheel, into the oscillators' frequency and pulse width
and the cutoff. **Aftertouch** can push the cutoff or the LFO.

## Mixer and Output

**Mixer:** levels of Osc A, Osc B and **Noise** (white or pink). **Output:** master tune and volume,
**Drift** (vintage per-voice spread), **Noise floor**, **Oversampling** (×2 or ×4 inside each
voice), and the voice setup — **5**, **10** or **16** voices, how voices are allocated, and unison
stacks.

## Presets

40 factory presets across strings and pads (**Glass Strings**, **Analog Strings**, **Warm Pad**,
**PWM Pad**, **Choir Vox**, **Sweep Pad**, **Solar Drift**), basses (**Pentad Bass**, **Sync Bass**,
**Unison Bass**, **Pluck Bass**, **Sub Drone**), leads (**Sync Lead**, **Unison Lead**, **Pulse
Lead**, **Screamer**, **Fifth Lead**), keys (**Poly Keys**, **Riley Organ**, **Clavi**, **Bell Keys**,
**Harpsi**), brass (**Poly Brass**, **Soft Horns**, **Brass Stab**, **Sync Brass**) and poly-mod
effects (**Poly-Mod Bells**, **Metallic Clang**, **Env Sync Sweep**, **Laser Zap**).

## Tips

- **Sync lead:** Sync on, sweep Osc A's pitch with the filter envelope through Poly-Mod.
- **Big chords:** 10 or 16 voices with Drift up a little — every note slightly different.
