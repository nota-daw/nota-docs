Nota Monolith is a monophonic synth laid out like the Minimoog Model D — Nota's own DSP, faithful
to how that instrument plays. Three oscillators, a mixer you can overdrive, a 24 dB ladder filter
and two contour generators: fat basses, singing leads, brass.

<Shot id="devices/instruments/monolith/card" alt="Nota Monolith, Controllers · Osc Bank tab" />

**Signal flow:** Osc 1 + Osc 2 + Osc 3 + noise (+ feedback) → mixer → ladder low-pass → VCA. Osc 3
or noise can modulate the oscillators or the filter through the mod wheel.

The **wheels** (pitch bend, mod wheel) are always on the left.

## Controllers · Osc Bank

| Section | Controls |
|---|---|
| **Controllers** | Master **Tune**, **Glide** (on / off and time), **Mod Mix** (Osc 3 ↔ noise as the modulation source), **Osc mod** and **Filter mod** switches |
| **Osc 1 / 2 / 3** | **Range** (LO, 32' … 2'), **Wave** (triangle, shark-tooth, saw, square, wide and narrow pulse — Osc 3 has reverse saw instead of shark-tooth), **Tune** ±7 semitones on Osc 2 and 3 |
| **Osc 3 kbd** | Off turns Osc 3 into a free-running LFO |

## Modifiers

<Shot id="devices/instruments/monolith/modifiers" alt="Nota Monolith, Modifiers tab with the filter and both contours" />

| Section | Controls |
|---|---|
| **Filter** | **Cutoff**, **Emphasis** (resonance — the ladder self-oscillates), **Contour** amount, keyboard tracking (⅓, ⅔) |
| **Filter contour** | Attack, Decay, Sustain |
| **Loudness contour** | Attack, Decay, Sustain |
| **Decay** | One switch for both contours: release follows decay, as on the original |

## Mixer

Level and on / off for each oscillator, **Noise** (white or pink) and **Ext** — the **Feedback**
path that overdrives the mixer for growl.

## Output

**Volume**, **Priority** (low, high or last note), **Legato** (single trigger), **Bend** range
(±2 … ±12), **Unison** voices with detune, **Drift** (gentle analog instability), **Bass
compensation**, velocity to amp and filter, and an **A-440 tone** for tuning.

## Presets

20 factory presets: basses (**Taurus Bass**, **Fat Bass**, **Reese Bass**, **Funk Square**,
**Rubber Bass**, **Sub Boom**, **Growl Overload**), leads (**Classic Lead**, **Hollow Lead**,
**Screaming Lead**, **Portamento**, **Bright Lead**, **Soft Flute**, **Whistle**), and more (**Pulse
Pad**, **Brass Section**, **Fifths Stack**, **Detune Unison**, **Sci-Fi Mod**, **Random Bleeps**).

## Tips

- **Classic lead:** Osc 2 and 3 a few cents apart from Osc 1, Emphasis halfway, a short filter
  contour, Glide on.
- **Growl:** raise the mixer **Feedback** until the ladder starts to bite.
