Nota Rhythm is a self-contained groovebox: eight drum voices and a 16-step sequencer that plays
in time with the transport. It starts on a factory kit; MIDI notes also trigger the voices live,
so you can finger-drum over the pattern.

<Shot id="devices/instruments/rhythm/card" alt="Nota Rhythm: the kit, the selected voice, Perform and the step strip" />

## The card, left to right

**Kit** — the eight voices: colour, name, an **fx** mark when it has effects, and **SYN** or **SMP**
(synth or sample). Click a voice to select it and hear it; drop an audio file on it to load a
sample; right-click for more. The header's preset picker chooses the **kit** — the same 25
factory kits as the [Drum Rack](../../racks/drum-rack/).

**Voice** — the selected voice, on two tabs:

- **Sound** — a synth voice shows its **hit** contour (drag across for Decay, up / down for
  Tune) and **Tune**, **Decay**, **Punch**, **Tone**, **Drive**, **Level**. A sample voice shows the
  file with its **Start** / end lines, **Reverse**, and Start · Length · Tune · Decay · Drive ·
  Level.
- **FX** — the voice's own effect chain: click a slot for the effect's card, add one from the
  menu or drop one in. A snare can have its own reverb, a kick its own saturation.

**Perform** — **Swing**, **Human** (velocity jitter) and **Accent**, then **Master** and **Glue** (a
gentle bus compressor on the whole kit), and the meter.

**Step** — the selected voice's 16 steps in bank **A**–**D**, with a velocity lane underneath.

## Programming steps

| Do this | To |
|---|---|
| Click a step | Turn it on or off |
| <Kbd>⇧</Kbd>-click | Accent it |
| <Kbd>⌥</Kbd>-click | Make it a quiet step |
| Drag across steps | Paint them |
| Drag a step up / down (or scroll) | Set its velocity |

The step menu fills every beat, every 8th or every 16th, adds off-beats, and shifts the pattern
left or right. Steps are 16ths: 16 steps = one bar.

The synth voices are a kick (sine with a pitch drop and click), snare (tone plus noise), clap,
rim, closed and open hats (the closed one chokes the open one, 808-style), tom and perc.

## Macros

<Shot id="devices/instruments/rhythm/macro" alt="Nota Rhythm, the kit's eight macros" />

**Macro** in the header swaps the panel for the kit's **eight macros**. Each drives one or more
targets — a voice's Decay, the Glue, or a parameter of a voice's effect — across a range, so one
knob can open every snare and clap reverb at once. Select a macro and **Map** a control to it;
right-click a macro for its targets and ranges, to rename it or clear it. A factory kit loads
with its own macros.

## Tips

- **A beat in a minute:** pick a kit from the header, select the kick, click steps 1, 5, 9 and 13,
  then the snare on 5 and 13, and raise **Swing** a little.
- **Bring in your own sounds:** drop one-shots from the browser onto the voices.
