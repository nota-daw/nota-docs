Nota Twin is a polyphonic analog synth built around one idea: **two sounds in one patch**. Every
sound control has a **side A** and a **side B**, and the **Morph** knob moves the whole sound from
one to the other — oscillators, filters, envelopes, effects and the depth of every modulation
route. Turn it slowly for an evolving pad, route it to pressure so each note morphs on its own, or
let the sequencer sweep it step by step.

<Shot id="devices/instruments/twin/card" alt="Nota Twin, Osc tab" />

**Signal flow per voice:** Osc 1 + Osc 2 + noise → the **Multimode** filter and the **Ladder** filter
(in series, in parallel or in between) → drive → amp. After the voices, each part has its own
effects: Mod FX → Delay → Reverb.

## Side A, side B and Morph

The **Morph** knob on the left is on screen on every tab. Under it, the strip shows where the sound
is now: the part as a bar, every sounding voice as a dot. **Edit A / B** picks the side the knobs
change — and so the side that automation and MIDI learn write to. An automation lane says which
side it drives: **Lower B · Ladder Cutoff**.

Each knob also shows the other side: a tick outside its groove marks the other side's value, and
while Morph sits between the two a dot marks the value you hear. Hover a knob for **A · B · now**.

- **A → B** and **B → A** copy one side onto the other, **Swap** exchanges them; each is one undo
  step.
- Stepped controls (a filter mode, a slope, the voice mode) switch halfway through the morph.
- Side B starts as a copy of side A, so a new patch doesn't change as you morph until you edit B.

## Osc

Two oscillators, each with **Wave** (triangle → saw → square, every shape in between), **Width**,
**Metal** (a wavefolder for bright, metallic tones), **Level**, **Coarse** and **Fine**, and a
**To filter** slider from *multi* to *ladder*: how much of it goes to each filter. Osc 2 adds **Sync** (Osc 2
follows Osc 1), **Low** (a slow modulator instead of a pitch), **FM 1** (Osc 2 into Osc 1's pitch)
and **FM cut** (Osc 2 into the multimode cutoff). **Noise** has its own level, colour and filter
balance.

## Filter

<Shot id="devices/instruments/twin/filter" alt="Nota Twin, Filter tab" />

The **Multimode** filter (low-pass, band-pass, high-pass and notch in between, in the Steiner
style; **Mode** is a slider across LP · BP · HP · notch) and the **Ladder** (12 or 24 dB, chosen in
its header; self-oscillating at high **Resonance**), each with **Cutoff**, envelope amount and
keytracking. **Route** slides from series (multimode into ladder) to parallel, and its diagram
shows the path. **Response** draws both filters and the combined curve. **Brute** feeds the output back into the filters for growl; **Drive** saturates them.

## Env and LFO

Three envelopes — **Filter env**, **Amp env** and **Mod env** (with **Delay** and **Loop**) — drawn
as graphs: drag the handles, or ⌥-click one to type its value. Under each graph, the **Attack**,
**Decay**, **Sustain** and **Release** values can be dragged too. **Velocity** sets how much the
velocity moves each; **Curve** bends the shape.

Three LFOs with six shapes (**Sine**, **Tri**, **Saw**, **Square**, **S&H**, **Smooth**), free or
tempo-synced, per voice or one for the whole part (**Voice** / **Global**), with fade-in.
**Vibrato** is LFO 1 on the pitch, opened by the mod wheel.

## Matrix

<Shot id="devices/instruments/twin/matrix" alt="Nota Twin, Matrix tab" />

Up to **64 routes** per part. A route is a source, a destination and a **depth on each side**, so
the depth morphs too. **+ Route** adds one; click a source or destination to change it, × removes
it.

- **Sources:** LFO 1–3, the three envelopes, velocity, release velocity (from a MIDI note-off),
  key, pressure, slide, bend, the mod and pitch wheels, **Pad X / Y / Z**, Random (a new value
  per note) and **Macro 1–8**.
- **Destinations:** Morph, pitch (both oscillators or one), vibrato, or any sound control.

A new patch starts with the expression routes: pressure and slide open the filters, **Pad X** moves
the multimode cutoff, **Pad Y** the Morph. Change or remove them like any other route. The eight
**Macros** beside the table are knobs to automate or map to a controller.

## Expression

Nota Twin plays per note: with a poly-aftertouch keyboard or an MPE controller, pressure, slide and
per-note bend move only the note you touch. Route pressure to **Morph** and each note morphs on its
own. The **Pad** on the Voice tab is a mouse-playable X · Y pad with a Z (pressure) ring; the
pitch-bend range (**Bend range**, ±2 to ±24) is on the same tab.

## FX

**Mod FX** (**Chorus**, **Flanger**, **Phaser** or **Ensemble**), **Delay** (free or synced, ping-pong)
and **Reverb**, each with its own **On**; an effect that is off greys out. They morph with the rest
of the sound. **All** · **On** switches all three off when you would rather use Nota's effect
devices.

## Seq

<Shot id="devices/instruments/twin/seq" alt="Nota Twin, Seq tab with a pattern" />

In the **Sequencer** header, turn **On** and pick **Seq** or **Arp**. Both follow the transport.

**Seq** plays one of 8 patterns of up to 64 steps. A step holds up to six notes, a velocity and a
gate — or a tie into the next step. Click a step to select it; drag its bar up or down for the
velocity and the bar along the bottom sideways for the gate (past the edge it ties into the next
step). **Rest** clears the selected step. Set the **Pattern**, its length, the step **Rate** and **Swing**. With **Transpose** on,
the key you hold moves the pattern (**Latch** keeps it moved); otherwise the keys you play sound
with it.

- **Record** — step record: play a chord, lift it, and it lands on the next step.
- **Lane 1–4** — motion lanes: each sets one control step by step (the Morph, the pad's X, Y or Z,
  or any sound control).
  **Rec motion** records the knob as the pattern passes; **Smooth** glides between steps.
- **To clip** writes the pattern as a MIDI clip at the playhead's bar and switches the sequencer off.

**Arp** plays the keys you hold: **Up**, **Down**, **Up/down**, **Random**, **As played** or **Chord**,
over 1–4 **Octaves**, with **Gate**, **Swing** and **Hold**.

The patterns are saved with the project and in presets, and each pattern edit is one undo step.

## Voice

**Poly**, **Mono**, **Legato** or **Unison** (with **Detune**), the number of **Voices** (4–12),
**Glide**, stereo **Spread** (by voice, by key or across the unison stack) and **Drift** — each
voice slightly different, the same way every time you render.

## Split and Layer

<Shot id="devices/instruments/twin/split" alt="Nota Twin in Split, Voice tab" />

Nota Twin has two parts, **Lower** and **Upper**, each a full Twin sound with its own sides, Morph,
matrix and effects. Pick the layout at the right of the tab row:

- **Single** — one sound, all the voices.
- **Layer** — both parts play every key.
- **Split** — keys below the split point play Lower, the rest play Upper.

In Layer and Split, **Lower / Upper** picks the part every tab edits. On the Voice tab, the
**Lower part** / **Upper part** section holds its **Volume**, **Pan** and **Octave**, the **Split**
key (**Learn**: play a key to set it) and the share of the **Voices** (2 + 10 to 10 + 2). On the Seq tab, **Plays** sends the
sequencer or the arpeggiator to Lower, Upper or both.

**Parts**, in the header of the Voice tab's **Output** (or part) section, copies Lower onto Upper
or back, swaps them, or loads a factory preset or one of your saved Twin presets into one part.
Loading into Upper of a Single patch turns it into a Layer with the current sound as Lower.

## Build a morphing pad from Init

A two-sided pad in a few minutes, with the card only:

1. Add Nota Twin, choose **Init** in the preset list and hold a chord.
2. **Env** tab: drag the **Amp env** attack and release handles out — about 1 s attack, 2 s release.
3. **Osc** tab: turn Osc 2's **Level** up and its **Fine** a little off centre for movement.
4. **Filter** tab: bring the **Ladder** **Cutoff** down to a warm, dark sound.
5. Switch **Edit** to **B**. Move both oscillators' **Wave** toward square, open the **Ladder**
   **Cutoff** and raise its **Resonance**; on the **FX** tab turn **Reverb** **On**.
6. Turn **Morph** slowly from 0 to 100 % — the pad travels from dark and soft to bright and
   wide. Automate Morph, or map it to the mod wheel on the **Matrix** tab (**Mod wheel** →
   **Morph**), to play the journey.

## Presets

57 factory presets in 11 folders: pads (**Warm Two-Filter Pad**, **Slow Bloom**, **Dream Morph
Pad**), keys (**Twin EP**, **Clav Funk**, **Drawbar Organ**), brass, bass (**Steiner Bass**, **Acid
Ladder**), leads (**Pressure Lead**, **Filter FM Scream**), plucks, strings (**String Machine**,
**Morphing Strings**), FX, sequences and arpeggios (**Berlin Bass Seq**, **Morph Sequence**, **Arp
Random Bells**), morphs that show the idea off (**Day to Night**, **Glass to Grit**, **Velocity
Morph**, **Wheel Morph**) and Split & Layer setups (**Bass + Pad Split**, **Keys + Strings Layer**,
**Arp Bass + Pad Split**). A preset with a sequence plays it when you preview it in the browser.

## Sizes, AI control

**L** shows every tab; **S** keeps Morph, the A / B switch, the pad and four knobs. Over MCP an AI
can read the patch with both sides (`read_twin`), set a side (`set_twin_morph_side`), copy sides or
parts (`copy_twin_side`, `copy_twin_part`), add routes by name (`set_twin_route`) and read, write
or bounce patterns (`get_twin_pattern`, `set_twin_pattern`, `twin_pattern_to_clip`).
