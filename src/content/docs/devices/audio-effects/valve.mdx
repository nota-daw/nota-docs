Nota Valve is an amp and cabinet: plug a guitar or bass straight in, or use it for grit on keys,
vocals and drums.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/valve/card" alt="Nota Valve, Amp tab with the tone stack response" />

**Signal flow:** pre-filter → **Bright** → preamp (oversampled) → tone stack → **Deep** → cabinet →
low / high cut → make-up → output → gate → mix.

**Layout:** the **Stage** column on the left (**Gain** and **Output** faders), a centre panel with
three tabs, a right panel (**Cabinet** / **Output**) and a status line.

## Amp

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Model | **Clean**, **Boost**, **Blues**, **Rock**, **Lead**, **Heavy**, **Bass** |
| **Gain** | Preamp drive |
| **Bass** · **Middle** · **Treble** · **Presence** | The tone stack; **Mid freq** sweeps the middle |
| **Bright** · **Deep** | Switches for extra top and extra low end |
| **Even only** | An even-harmonics-only preamp — warm instead of gritty |

The graph shows the tone stack's response against flat.

## Cab

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/valve/cab" alt="Nota Valve, Cab tab with the cabinet response" />

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Cabinet | **Match** (follows the model), **1×12 Open**, **2×12 Combo**, **4×12 Closed**, **1×15 Bass** |
| Mic | **Dyn** (dynamic), **Cond** (condenser) or **Ribbon** |
| **Distance** · **Axis** · position | How far the mic is, on or off axis, at the **Cap** or the **Edge** of the speaker |
| **Low cut** · **High cut** | Trim the cabinet's extremes |

The graph shows this cabinet's response against the mic on axis at the cap.

## Harmonics

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/valve/harmonics" alt="Nota Valve, Harmonics tab" />

The harmonics the preamp adds right now — see how Gain and the model change the character.

## Output

**Output**, **Mix** (dry / wet), **Auto comp** (keeps the level steady as Gain changes), the **Gate**
(keyed on the input, to stop hiss between notes), **Oversampling**, and the cabinet on / off.

## Presets

31 factory presets: clean (**Clean Combo**, **Jazz Box**, **Glassy Clean**, **Funk Snap**, **Country
Twang**), blues (**Blues Breakup**, **Edge of Breakup**, **Texas Blues**, **Smooth Blues Lead**,
**Even Warmth**), rock (**Rock Crunch**, **Classic Stack**, **Arena Rhythm**, **Indie Jangle**,
**Garage Fuzz**), lead (**Lead Sustain**, **Singing Lead**, **Shred Lead**), heavy (**Heavy Chug**,
**Modern Metal**, **Doom Sludge**, **Djent Tight**), bass (**Bass Amp**, **Bass Growl**, **Bass DI
Clean**, **Bass Drive Blend**) and beyond guitar (**Lo-Fi Radio Amp**, **Keys Grit**, **Vocal Tube
Color**, **Drum Crunch**, **Parallel Grind**).
