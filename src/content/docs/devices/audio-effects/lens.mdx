Nota Lens shows what a sound is made of. It changes nothing — put it anywhere in a chain, or on the
master, to look at the spectrum, the waveform or how the sound evolves over time.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/lens/card" alt="Nota Lens, Spectrum view" />

**Layout:** one large graph with three views — **Spectrum** (Smooth, Decay and Tilt beside it, the
FFT size and Mid / Side under it), **Scope** (Time/Div, Volt/Div and the trigger level beside it, Sync,
the trigger or span and Afterglow under it) and **Waterfall** (Gain, Floor and Contrast beside it, the
speed under it) — with the source (**L+R** / **L** / **R**) and **Freeze** in the tab bar, and a right
panel that follows the view: the spectrum's peak hold, window, averaging, floor and scale with the
peak / crest / LUFS read-out and the cursor; the scope's **Cursors** switch and layout, holdoff, key,
afterglow settings and the capture read-out (the cursors' measurements while Cursors is on); the
waterfall's window, FFT, overlap, averaging and frame rate. An analyzer has no output stage, so there
are no output knobs. The **S / L** switch in the header folds the card to **S**: the spectrum over
the source and **Smooth**, **Decay**, **Tilt** and **Floor**.

## Spectrum

An FFT spectrum on a log frequency scale: **FFT** size and window, **Avg**, smoothing, decay, a
**Tilt** (so a balanced mix looks flat), a floor, and **peak hold**. **Mid/Side** shows the mid or the
side instead of left / right.

## Scope

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/lens/scope" alt="Nota Lens, Scope view" />

A triggered oscilloscope that behaves like a bench instrument: the trace is pinned to the trigger
point and stands still, older passes fade (**Afterglow**). **Free** runs untriggered; a **Single**
capture holds one sweep. **Cursors** A and B measure time and level between two points. **Export
WAV** saves the captured window.

### Tempo sync

Turn on **Sync** above the scope to line it up with the song instead of a trigger. Pick a **Span**:
¼ beat, ½ beat, 1 beat, 2 beats, 1 bar, 2 bars or 4 bars (a bar follows the time signature). While
the song plays, the scope shows that stretch of the beat grid: a 1 bar span starts on a bar line and
ends on the next one. Beat lines are drawn across it, bar lines stronger, and the labels underneath
give the bar and beat at each edge.

A sweep line follows the playhead. Left of it is the pass being drawn now; right of it, the previous
pass fades out, so you always see a full span. This makes it easy to see whether a note's attack sits
on the beat or just after it, bar after bar.

- Long spans are drawn from a peak history, so a click or a transient keeps its full height even at 4
  bars.
- A seek or a loop wrap starts the pass again at the new position, instead of
  drawing old audio under the new beat lines.
- Stopped, the scope rolls freely on a track that still sounds (an instrument you play, or a
  monitored input), with **free** in the corner. A stopped audio track is silent, so its scope keeps
  the last pass.
- With Sync on, the time base and trigger knobs rest. **Freeze** holds both passes as they are.

### Key overlay

Lens takes a sidechain key, like the Compressor. In **Key** on the Scope panel, pick another
track: its audio (after its effects) is drawn in teal over this track's trace, in the synced scope
and in the triggered one, where it follows the same trigger and time base. The switch next to it
hides the key's trace.

- **Key Gain** (−24 to +24 dB) scales only the key's trace, to compare a quiet key with a loud
  signal. It changes nothing you hear.
- **Layout** puts both traces on one axis (**Overlay**) or the key in the lower half (**Stacked**).
- **Source** and **Mid/Side** pick the channel for both traces.

A typical use: put Lens on the bass, key it from the kick, set Sync to 1 bar, and see whether the
bass ducks where the kick hits.

### Bigger window

The button at the right of the view tabs opens Lens in its own window, which you can resize or move
to another display. It shows the same views and controls as the card, and both follow each other.
The window closes when you delete the device or open another project.

## Waterfall

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/lens/waterfall" alt="Nota Lens, Waterfall view" />

The spectrum over time, scrolling — see resonances ring, reverb tails decay and drum patterns repeat.
**Contrast** and brightness set how much detail shows.

## Common controls

**Freeze** stops the display; **Source** picks what is analysed; the scale's top and range set the
vertical view. The status line carries loudness and the measurements.
