Nota Shutter opens and closes a signal. As a **gate** it cleans up noise and bleed between hits;
flipped, it **ducks** one sound under another; and its **Pattern** tab turns it into a rhythmic,
tempo-synced gate you draw.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/shutter/card" alt="Nota Shutter, Signal tab with the input and the gated output" />

## The gate

The shutter **opens** when the level crosses the **Threshold** and **closes** when it falls back past
the **Return** — the gap stops it chattering.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Threshold** · **Return** | Open and close levels |
| **Attack** · **Hold** · **Release** | Shape each opening |
| Shape | **Linear**, **Log** (the classic) or **Snap** (stays open, then shuts hard) |
| **Floor** | How far the closed signal drops — −∞ mutes, higher leaves some through |
| **Lookahead** | Opens just before a transient |
| **Flip** | Turns the gate into a ducker |
| **Retrigger** | Every hit fires one attack → hold → release envelope (trigger mode) |

## Envelope

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/shutter/envelope" alt="Nota Shutter, Envelope tab with one opening drawn" />

One opening drawn from attack, hold, release, shape and floor.

## Sidechain

The detector listens to this track or an **External sidechain** (another track), through its own
band-pass **Detector filter**, with **Listen** to hear it and **Peak hold**. Key a gate from the kick
to chop a pad; duck a bass under the kick.

## Pattern

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/shutter/pattern" alt="Nota Shutter, Pattern tab with a 16-step gate pattern" />

A tempo-synced step pattern of gain — a **rhythmic gate**:

- Draw steps with **Draw**, **Line**, **Erase** and **Flip** on an **8**, **16** or **32**-step grid.
- Flip through pattern shapes, or roll a new one with the dice and **Complexity**.
- **Smooth** ramps into each step, **Swing** delays the off-steps.
- **Rate** runs from a quarter note to 4 bars in **Sync** (or 4 to 0.25 Hz in **Free**); **Depth** sets
  how far an empty step drops.
- A MIDI note on the track can restart the pattern; **Gated** plays it only while the threshold gate
  is open.
- **Target**: **Volume** (it gates the audio) or **Map** — the audio stays untouched and the pattern
  becomes a CV source for the [Modular view](../../../views/modular/).

Every step and pattern setting can be automated.
