Nota Level measures loudness the way streaming and broadcast do (ITU-R BS.1770) and rides a gain
so the output sits on a **target** in LUFS. Use it to level a voice, prepare a podcast, stage gain
before a chain, or match one track to another.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/level/card" alt="Nota Level: the correction, 8 seconds of loudness against the target, the meters" />

## Correction

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Auto** / **Manual** | Auto rides the gain toward the target; Manual applies the fader you set |
| **Match** | In Manual: set the gain to the distance to the target, once |
| **Reset** | In Auto: restart the measurement from now |
| **Target** | The loudness to reach — click the value for standards (streaming, broadcast…) or a **reference track** |
| **Trim** | An offset on top |

The applied gain shows in large type, with the reason when it's held (max gain, true peak).
Pauses and release tails don't get pumped up: while the input is too quiet to level, the gain holds.

## The graph

**Scale** — **Mom** (momentary, 0.4 s), **Short** (3 s) or **Integ** (integrated, gated) — over 8
seconds of input, output and the target with its ±1 LU band. Drag the target line.

## Response and safety

**Fast** / **Slow**, **Window** (how much loudness history it averages), **Max Gain** (the most it
will ever change). **True-peak safe** — a look-ahead limiter keeps the output under the **Ceiling**
in dBTP; its delay is compensated.

## Matching a reference

Pick a reference track as the target (it becomes the device's sidechain source): Level then
matches this track's loudness to that one, on the same scale.
