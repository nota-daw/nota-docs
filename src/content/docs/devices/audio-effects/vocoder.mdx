Nota Vocoder makes one sound speak with another. The track you put it on is the **voice**
(the modulator): its level in each frequency band opens the same band of the **carrier**. Drop
it on a vocal and you hear a robot voice straight away; pick a pad as the carrier and the pad
says the words. Robot voices, vocoded choirs, talk-box leads, whispers.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/vocoder/card" alt="Nota Vocoder: the modulator and carrier, the envelope graph over the band knobs, and the output" />

## Modulator

The modulator is always **this track** — usually a vocal.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Gain** | Trims the voice before the bands hear it, ±24 dB |
| **Key** | The voice's level after Gain |

## Carrier

| Choice | Sound |
|---|---|
| **Saw** | An internal saw at **Pitch** (C0–C7, C3 by default) — the default, a robot voice |
| **Noise** | White noise, for a whisper |
| **Track** | Another track, picked below — a pad or a synth that speaks the words |

With **Saw** and **Noise** the result follows the voice's level. With **Track** it sits at that
track's level whatever the voice's; the voice's pauses still close the bands.

In an Instrument or Audio Effect Rack chain the Vocoder has no sidechain: use **Saw** or **Noise**
there (with **Track** it passes the chain's sound through unchanged).

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Track picker** | The carrier track, with its colour. Every other track and return is offered; this track and the groups it sits in are not (their sound contains the vocoder's own output). Picking one switches the carrier to **Track**; **None** leaves this track playing unchanged until you pick |
| **Pre** / **Post** | Where the carrier track is tapped: before its effects and fader, or after both. With **Pre**, mute the carrier track to hear only the vocoder — it keeps feeding the bands |
| **Tilt** | Brightens or darkens Saw or Track around 1 kHz before the bands, ±12 dB; a dull pad speaks more clearly with a little more top. Inactive on Noise |
| **Pitch** | The saw's note. Active on Saw only |

## Bands

The graph shows one bar per band — the modulator's level in it — and a teal line for the
carrier's level in the same bands. The two upright lines are the edges of the band range: drag
one to move **Low** or **High**, double-click it to reset.

| Control | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Bands** | 8–32 | Fewer is more robotic, more is clearer |
| **Low** | 20 Hz – 1 kHz | The lowest band's edge |
| **High** | 2–20 kHz | The highest band's edge |
| **Q** | 1–10 | How narrow each band is |
| **Formant** | ±12 st | Moves the carrier's bands against the modulator's: up sounds smaller, down larger. The pitch stays |
| **Attack** | 0.5–100 ms | How fast a band opens |
| **Release** | 5–1000 ms | How fast a band closes; longer is smoother, shorter more articulate |

## Output

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Unvoiced** | Adds high noise where the modulator hisses (s, t, sh), so consonants stay clear |
| **Mix** | This track as it came in against the vocoded sound |
| **Gain** | Output level, ±12 dB |
| **Hold** | Freezes every band where it is; the carrier keeps sounding through it |

The meter at the foot of the column is the output level.

## Presets

Seven factory presets: voices (**Init**, **Robot choir**, **Talk box**, **Whisper**, **Lo-fi robot**)
and pads (**Strings choir**, **Deep giant**). **Talk box** and **Strings choir** use a carrier
track: pick one after loading them. A preset never names a track. In the browser, those two
preview by vocoding the demo phrase with itself.

## Agents

Over MCP the vocoder is an ordinary audio effect (kind 26). For a carrier track, set its
**Carrier** param to Track and route the track with `set_device_sidechain`.
