Nota EQ-8 is a mixing-console equalizer with eight bands. Use it to clean up mud, cut rumble,
add air, carve space for one sound around another — on the stereo pair, the mid or the side.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/eq-8/card" alt="Nota EQ-8 with the band chips, the response graph and the band panel" />

## The graph

Each band is a numbered node on the response curve — drag it: sideways for frequency, up and
down for gain. The band chips above the graph switch bands on and off and select one for the
panel on the right. **Analyzer** in the corner shows the spectrum **Pre** or **Post** EQ, or
**Off**.

## A band

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Type | **Low cut**, **Low shelf**, **Bell**, **Notch**, **High shelf**, **High cut** |
| **Freq** · **Gain** · **Q** | Where, how much, how wide (on the cuts, **Reso**) |
| **Slope** | For the cuts: 12, 24 or 48 dB per octave |
| **Channel** | **St** (both channels), **Mid**, **Side**, **L** or **R** — so a band can brighten only the sides, or tame one channel |

## Global

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Scale** | Multiplies every shelf and bell gain at once, 0–200 % — 50 % halves the whole curve |
| **Output** | Output trim |
| **Auto gain** | Compensates the average level change of the shelves and bells, so you judge an edit at the same loudness |

## Presets

34 factory presets: tone (**Bass Boost**, **Air & Presence**, **Smile Curve**, **Loudness Contour**,
**Master Tilt**), cleanup (**Low Cut**, **Sub Rumble Filter**, **Mud Cut**, **Hum Removal 50 Hz** /
**60 Hz**, **Mono Low End**), mix (**Clean Mix Bus**, **Pad Tuck**, **Double-Track Split**, **Wide
Air**, **Focus Center**), vocals (**Vocal Presence**, **De-Box Vocal**, **Sibilance Tamer**,
**Podcast Voice**), drums (**Kick Punch**, **Snare Crack**, **Overheads Air**, **Hi-Hat Clean**),
instruments (**Bass Definition**, **Acoustic Guitar**, **Electric Guitar Cab**, **Piano Clarity**)
and colour (**Telephone**, **Lo-Fi Radio**, **Band Pass**).

## Tips

- **Cut narrow, boost wide:** find a harsh frequency with a narrow, boosted bell, then flip it
  to a cut.
- **Mono lows:** a low cut on the **Side** band around 120 Hz keeps the bass centred.
