Nota Delay repeats the sound: slapback, rhythmic eighths, dub throws, tape echoes, frozen beds.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/delay/card" alt="Nota Delay, Time tab over the live repeat window" />

**Layout:** the **Loop** column on the left (**Feedback**, **Spread**), a centre panel with two tabs
over a live window of the repeats, a right panel (**Levels** / **Output**) and a status line that
tells you how many repeats stay audible.

## Time

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sync** / **ms** | Time as a note division (1/16 … 1/2, including triplets and dotted) or in milliseconds (up to 2 s) |
| **L time** · **R time** | The two channels' times |
| **Link** | The right channel follows the left |
| **Ping** | Ping-pong — each repeat crosses to the other side |
| **Fade on change** | Changing the time crossfades to the new one instead of gliding (no pitch bend) |
| **Tap tempo** | Tap four times to set the time by ear (switches to ms) |

## Loop · Wow

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/delay/loop-wow" alt="Nota Delay, Loop · Wow tab" />

The feedback loop: a **low cut** and **high cut** filter (drag its handles), **Diffuse** (smears the
repeats into a cloud), **Tape mode** (saturation), and tape **Wow** — **Wow rate** and **Wow depth**.
**Freeze** holds the loop forever: the input is muted and the repeats stop decaying. **Clear
loop** empties the buffer.

## Levels and Output

**Dry**, **Mix**, **Width**, **Mono f** (bass mono), **Wet only** (for a return track), **Out**, and
**Latency comp.** — keeps the echo exactly on time when Diffuse is up.

## Presets

30 factory presets: short (**Slapback**, **Doubler**, **Rockabilly Slap**, **Haas Widener**, **Slap
Wide**), rhythmic (**Eighth Bounce**, **Dub Eighths**, **Ping Quarter**, **Dotted Eighth**, **Triplet
Roll**, **Sixteenth Stutter**, **Half-Note Wash**, **Cross Rhythm**, **Polyrhythm 3:4**, **Wide
Quarters**, **Trance Gate Echo**), tape (**Tape Echo**, **Space Echo**, **Warped Tape**, **Bucket
Brigade**, **Repitch Riser**), colour and space (**Vocal Throw**, **Telephone Echo**, **Diffuse
Cloud**, **Ambient Wash**, **Dark Cavern**, **Tape Hold**, **Frozen Bed**) and for returns (**Send ·
Eighth**, **Send · Quarter**).

## Tips

- **Dub throw:** automate the send to a Delay return on the last word of a phrase.
- **Wide without echo:** 10–25 ms on one side only (**Haas Widener**).
