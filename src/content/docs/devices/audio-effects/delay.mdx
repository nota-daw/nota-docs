Nota Delay repeats the sound: slapback, rhythmic eighths, dub throws, tape echoes, frozen beds.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/delay/card" alt="Nota Delay, Time tab with the live echoes and where the next repeats land" />

**Layout:** the **Loop** column on the left (**Feedback**, **Spread**), a centre panel with two tabs,
each one live graph of your audio that fills it, a right panel with the controls of the tab you're
on (or **Output**), and a status line that tells you how many repeats stay audible.

| Centre tab | Right panel |
|---|---|
| **Time** | ms / Sync, Link, Ping, Fade, the L and R times, Feedback, Spread, Tap tempo, Clear loop |
| **Loop · Wow** | Feedback, Diffuse, Low cut, High cut, Wow rate, Wow, Freeze, Tape |

## Time

The last seconds of your audio: the input as a grey wash and the echoes as a line, on the project's
beat grid while it plays in **Sync**. The dots after the latest hit show where the next repeats will
land and how loud they'll be — accent for the left channel, teal for the right.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sync** / **ms** | Time as a note division (1/16 … 1/2, including triplets and dotted) or in milliseconds (up to 2 s) |
| **L time** · **R time** | The two channels' times |
| **Link** | The right channel follows the left |
| **Ping** | Ping-pong — each repeat crosses to the other side |
| **Fade** | Changing the time crossfades to the new one instead of gliding (no pitch bend) |
| **Tap tempo** | Tap four times to set the time by ear (switches to ms) |

## Loop · Wow

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/delay/loop-wow" alt="Nota Delay, Loop · Wow tab" />

How each of the first six repeats sounds: every pass round the loop goes through the **low cut**,
the **high cut** and (in **Tape** mode) the tape's head loss, and loses the feedback, so each curve sits
lower and narrower than the last. Drag the cut handles, double-click to open them. The grey line
underneath is the echoes' live spectrum.

**Diffuse** smears the repeats into a cloud, **Tape** adds saturation, and tape **Wow** — **Wow rate**
and **Wow** depth — bends the pitch. Right of the tabs: the filter range and, once Wow
bends the pitch audibly, by how many cents. **Freeze** holds the loop
forever: the input is muted and the repeats stop decaying. **Clear loop** empties the buffer.

## Output

**Dry**, **Mix**, **Width**, **Mono** (bass mono), **Wet only** (for a return track), **Out**, and
**Latency comp.** — keeps the echo exactly on time when Diffuse is up.

## Presets

30 factory presets: short (**Slapback**, **Doubler**, **Rockabilly Slap**, **Haas Widener**, **Slap
Wide**), rhythmic (**Eighth Bounce**, **Dub Eighths**, **Ping Quarter**, **Dotted Eighth**, **Triplet
Roll**, **Sixteenth Stutter**, **Half-Note Wash**, **Cross Rhythm**, **Polyrhythm 3:4**, **Wide
Quarters**, **Trance Gate Echo**), tape (**Tape Echo**, **Space Echo**, **Warped Tape**, **Bucket
Brigade**, **Repitch Riser**), colour and space (**Vocal Throw**, **Telephone Echo**, **Diffuse
Cloud**, **Ambient Wash**, **Dark Cavern**, **Tape Hold**, **Frozen Bed**) and for returns (**Send ·
Eighth**, **Send · Quarter**).

## Tips

- **Dub throw:** automate the send to a Delay return on the last word of a phrase.
- **Wide without echo:** 10–25 ms on one side only (**Haas Widener**).
