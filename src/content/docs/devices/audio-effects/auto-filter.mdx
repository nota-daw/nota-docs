Nota Auto Filter is a filter with built-in motion: an **envelope** opens it as the sound gets
louder (auto-wah, envelope filter), an **LFO** sweeps it in time (wobble, gates, slow motion).

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/auto-filter/card" alt="Nota Auto Filter, Filter tab with the response" />

**Layout:** the **Shape** column on the left (**Cutoff** and **Reso** faders, with the modulated value
marked), a centre panel with three tabs — each one window where you see the cutoff move — a right
panel and a status line.

## Filter

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Type | **LP**, **BP**, **HP** or **Notch** |
| **Morph** | Blends continuously between the types |
| Slope | 12 or 24 dB |
| **Freq** · **Reso** | Cutoff and resonance — or drag the response |
| **Drive** | Pre-filter drive for colour |
| Circuit | **Clean** or **Analog** (saturating) |

## Envelope

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/auto-filter/envelope" alt="Nota Auto Filter, Envelope tab with the cutoff over time" />

An envelope follower: **Amount** (up or down), **Attack**, **Release** and **Hold** (a time, or
infinite). It listens to the track itself or to a **key** — another track picked as the device's
sidechain source. The window draws the cutoff over time.

## LFO

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/auto-filter/lfo" alt="Nota Auto Filter, LFO tab" />

**Amount**, **Rate** (**Free** in Hz or synced to the tempo), wave — **Sine**, **Tri**, **Saw**,
**Sqr**, **S&H** — with **Morph**, start offset, a stereo phase offset between the channels, and
retrigger. The window draws two bars of it.

## Output

**→ Freq** / **→ Reso** — what the modulation moves: the cutoff, the resonance, or both. **Mod smooth** slews it,
**Gain** trims, **Dry/Wet** blends.

## Presets

30 factory presets: static filters (**Clean Sweep**, **24 dB Low Cut**, **Telephone**, **Warm Analog
LP**, **Resonant Peak**, **Notch Carve**), envelope (**Envelope Wah**, **Clav Wah**, **Funk Quack**,
**Bass Auto-Wah**, **Reverse Wah**, **Drum Snap**, **Swell Opener**, **Resonance Bloom**, **Hold &
Release**, **Talking Filter**), LFO (**Slow LFO Sweep**, **Pad Motion**, **Wobble 1/8**, **Dubstep Wub
1/16**, **Trance Gate**, **S&H Random**, **Glide Random**, **Saw Ramp Bar**, **Stereo Swirl**, **Notch
Motion**, **Phaser-ish Notch**, **Reso Pulse**) and sidechain (**Sidechain Duck**, **Kick Pump HP**).
