Nota Dynamic EQ-8 is an eight-band EQ in which every band can also behave like a small compressor
or expander for just its frequencies. Tame sibilance only when it happens, dip the bass only when
the kick hits, lift air only when a vocal goes quiet.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/dynamic-eq/card" alt="Nota Dynamic EQ-8 with the response graph and the band panel" />

## A band

The static half is the same as [EQ-8](../eq-8/): type, **Freq**, **Gain**, **Q**. The dynamic half:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Mode | **Static** — a plain EQ band; **Duck** — acts as the band gets louder than the threshold; **Lift** — acts as it falls below |
| **Thresh** | The level where it starts to act; the band's current level is marked on it |
| **Range** | How many dB at full action — negative cuts, positive boosts (a Duck that boosts is upward expansion) |
| **Attack** · **Release** | How fast it acts and lets go |
| **Key** | **Self** (the band's own signal) or **Ext** (another track — the device's sidechain source) |
| **Solo** | Hear only this band |

Only shelves and bells take dynamic gain. Full action is reached 6 dB past the threshold. The band
chips above the graph light up with each band's activity.

## Global

**Dynamic** switches all dynamics off at once — an instant A/B of what they do. **Out** trims the
output.

## Presets

33 factory presets: vocals (**De-Ess**, **De-Ess Soft**, **De-Harsh Vox**, **Vocal Tame**, **Vocal
Presence**, **Proximity Control**, **Air Lift Vocal**, **Podcast Voice**), mix and master (**Mix Bus
Air**, **Mud Control**, **Harshness Guard**, **Warm Master**, **Master Glue EQ**, **Loudness
Contour**), drums (**Punch Tighten**, **Kick Box Cut**, **Snare Ring Tamer**, **Cymbal Smoother**,
**Room Mic Duck**), bass (**Bass Control**, **Bass Clarity**, **Sub Tamer**, **Kick Ducks Bass**) and
more (**Vocal Pocket**, **Voiceover Space**, **Guitar Resonance**, **Piano Boom Control**, **Pad
Smoother**, **Upward Air**, **Hiss Dip**).

## Tips

- **Make room for the vocal:** on a pad, a Duck bell around 2–3 kHz keyed **Ext** from the vocal
  track.
- **Kick and bass:** on the bass, a Duck low shelf keyed from the kick (**Kick Ducks Bass**).
