Nota Ceiling makes a track or a master loud without ever going over: nothing passes the
**Ceiling**. It also measures loudness the way streaming services do, so you can tell whether a
master is ready to deliver without another plug-in.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/ceiling/card" alt="Nota Ceiling, Level tab with input, the part over the ceiling and output" />

**Signal flow:** **Gain** drives the signal into the ceiling; a look-ahead lets the gain duck
*before* each peak arrives; release recovers; a final safety clip guarantees no sample passes.

## Limiting

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Gain** | Drives into the limiter — louder means more limiting |
| **Ceiling** | The maximum output level — drag it on the Level graph |
| **Release** · auto release | How fast it recovers; auto slows as the reduction deepens |
| **Character** | **Clean**, **Punch** or **Glue** (with a soft clip) |
| **Look** | Look-ahead time |
| **Link** | From a glued centre to independent channels |
| **True Peak** | Measures inter-sample peaks too, so a DAC or a codec never clips |
| **SC HP** | Keeps the lows out of the detector, so the bass stops pumping the mix |
| **Delta** | Hear what the limiter takes away |

The detector can also listen to another track (**Internal** or the device's sidechain source).

## The graphs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Level** | Input, the part over the ceiling and the output over 4 seconds, with the reduction under it |
| **Reduction** | The reduction and the transients that reached the clip |
| **Loudness** | Momentary, short-term and integrated loudness over 60 seconds around the **Target** — drag it |

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/ceiling/loudness" alt="Nota Ceiling, Loudness tab with LUFS against the target" />

## Meters

**LUFS-M**, **LUFS-S**, **LUFS-I** (integrated), loudness range (LRA), true peak and PLR, against the
**Target** you set.

## Presets

32 factory presets: mastering targets (**Master Safe**, **Transparent Safety**, **Loud Master**,
**Streaming −1**, **Streaming −14**, **Apple Music −16**, **Podcast −16**, **Broadcast R128 −23**,
**Club Master −8**, **CD Brickwall**, **Gentle Master**, **Vinyl Pre-Master**, **Bass-Heavy Master**,
**Headroom −6**), bus and drums (**Drum Punch**, **Drum Bus**, **Glue Bus**, **Mix Bus Glue**, **Kick
Clipper**, **Snare Crack**, **808 Safety**), instruments (**Vocal Peak Catcher**, **Guitar Wall**,
**Synth Lead Tamer**) and colour (**Soft Clip Warmth**, **Pumping Smash**, **Brickwall Crush**, **Lo-Fi
Squash**, **Hear the Squash**, **Live Zero Latency**, **Wide Mix Unlinked**).
