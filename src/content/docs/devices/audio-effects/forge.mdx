Nota Forge is three saturators in one — tape, tube, diode, fuzz, folding — that you can stack,
blend, split into mid and side, or give each a frequency band.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/forge/card" alt="Nota Forge: routing and the three stages, the transfer curve and harmonics, the stage shape" />

## Routing

| Routing | How the three stages combine |
|---|---|
| **Serial** | 1 → 2 → 3 |
| **Parallel** | All three from the same input, averaged |
| **M/S** | Stage 1 on the mid, stage 2 on the side, stage 3 on the recombined stereo |
| **Multi** | Stage 1 on the lows (under 180 Hz), stage 2 the mids, stage 3 the highs (over 2.4 kHz) — a stage that's off lets its band through clean |

## A stage

On the left, each stage has its on dot, its type, its role in the routing, an output trim (drag
the dB), **Drive** and a teal **Feedback**. Click a stage to edit its **shape** on the right:
**Bias** (asymmetry — even harmonics), **Tone** (a ±12 dB tilt after it) and **Width** (its stereo
image, 0–200 %).

## The centre

**Amount** (drive into the chain), **Wet** and **Out** over the transfer curve — the whole device,
each stage faintly, the LFO-modulated curve dashed — and the harmonics of a −6 dB sine with its THD.
Drag the node on the curve for Amount.

## Modulation

**LFO → Drive** (free or synced, with **Rate**) makes the drive breathe; **Env → Tone** opens the tone
as the signal gets louder. **Oversample** reduces aliasing on hard settings.

## Presets

32 factory presets: warmth (**Cabinet Heat**, **Tape Glue**, **Tube Warmth**, **Console Bus**, **Vocal
Silk**, **Bass Weight**, **Parallel Warmth**, **Warm Master**), drive (**Diode Crunch**, **Amp Stack**,
**Guitar Edge**, **Snare Crack**, **Fuzz Wall**, **Digital Hard Clip**, **Broken Speaker**, **Feedback
Scream**), folding (**Fold Bells**, **Wavefolder Synth**), bus and parallel (**Drum Glue**, **NY Drums**,
**Bass Parallel Grit**), mid / side (**Wide Air**, **Mono Grit Core**, **Side Sizzle**), multiband
(**Multiband Drive**, **Low-End Only**, **Presence Lift**) and motion (**Breathing Drive**, **Wobble
Crunch**, **Envelope Bite**, **Slow Tide**).
