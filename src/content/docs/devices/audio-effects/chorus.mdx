Nota Chorus thickens and widens a sound with slightly delayed, gently detuned copies of it. Wide
synths, shimmering guitars, string machines, vocal doubles.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/chorus/card" alt="Nota Chorus: the LFO and mode, the voices over two cycles and the voice controls" />

## Modes

| Mode | Voices | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Classic** | Two — left and right, 8 ms ± 5 ms | The classic stereo chorus; **Offset** (0–180°) sets how far the right voice's LFO runs behind |
| **Ensemble** | Three, 120° apart — left, centre, right; 12 ms ± 6 ms | The lush string-machine sound |
| **Vibrato** | The Classic pair on a short line, 3 ms ± 2.5 ms, wet only | Pitch wobble without the dry signal |

## LFO

A sine, **Hz** (free, 0.02–8 Hz) or **Sync** (4 bars to 1/16, with triplets), and **Rate**. **HPF**
keeps the lows out of the wet signal (off, or 20 Hz–2 kHz), so the bass stays solid.

## The window

Every voice over two cycles — as its delay (**τ**) or its detune in cents (**ct**) — with a running
head; drag up and down for **Amount**. Underneath, the voices' stereo field.

## Voice

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Amount** | Depth of the modulation |
| **Feedback** | Bipolar, ±90 % — towards flanging and ringing |
| **Width** | 0 % mono · 100 % as is · 200 % extra side |
| **Warmth** | A bucket-brigade colour: darker and gently saturated |
| **Mix** | Dry / wet (locked to wet in Vibrato) |

The column also shows the detune and delay ranges you're getting.
