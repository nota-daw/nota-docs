Nota Beat Repeat grabs a short slice of what's playing and repeats it, in time — snare rolls, end-of-bar
fills, vocal chops, glitchy stutters.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/beat-repeat/card" alt="Nota Beat Repeat, Timeline tab with capture and repeats over two intervals" />

**How it works:** every **Interval** (shifted by **Offset**) it rolls the dice — **Chance**. When it
fires, it captures a **Grid**-long slice and loops it until the **Gate** ends, each repeat pitched,
faded and filtered.

## Timing

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Interval** · **Offset** | How often it may fire, and where in the bar |
| **Grid** | Slice length; **Triplet** turns the grid into triplets |
| **Variation** | Lets the grid float a few steps per trigger |
| **Chance** | Probability of firing at each interval |
| **Gate** | How long the repeating lasts |
| **Repeat** · **Latch** | Force a repeat now, as long as it's held (or latched on) |

The **Timeline** tab shows capture and repeats over two intervals; **Slices** shows the interval's
sound with the offset, gate and slice on it — drag them.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/beat-repeat/slices" alt="Nota Beat Repeat, Slices tab" />

## Character

**Pitch** (and **Pitch decay** per repeat — a dive), **Decay** (each repeat quieter), **Volume**, and
the **Filter**: on / off, **LP**, **BP** or **HP**, **Freq** and width, optionally **narrowing with
repeats**.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/beat-repeat/filter" alt="Nota Beat Repeat, Filter tab" />

## Output

Mode: **Mix** (repeats over the dry), **Insert** (repeats replace the dry while they play) or **Gate**
(only the bursts sound); and **Mix** (dry / wet).

## Presets

31 factory presets: stutters (**Classic Stutter**, **Half-Bar Roll**, **Snare Stutter**, **End-of-Bar
Fill**, **Build-Up Roll**, **Stutter Mix**, **Glitch Gate**, **Triplet Tumble**), random (**Random
Glitch**, **Micro Buzz**, **Buffer Shuffle**, **Sparse Surprise**), pitch (**Pitch Drop**, **Tape
Drop**, **Octave Up Flutter**, **Fifth Echo**, **Dive Bomb**, **Chipmunk Roll**), filtered (**Filtered
Chops**, **Telephone Repeats**, **Low-Pass Fade**, **High-Pass Riser**, **Radio Stutter**), by
instrument (**Hat Roll**, **Kick Stutter**, **Vocal Chop**, **Vocal Echo Tail**) and performance
(**Hold to Stutter**, **Latch Freeze**, **Gate Only Bursts**).

## Tips

- **Play it live:** map **Repeat** to a pad or a key with MIDI Learn.
- Beat Repeat needs the transport running to follow the bar.
