Nota Chamber is two reverbs in one: a **convolution** engine that plays back real-sounding spaces,
and an **algorithmic** engine for lush, modulated, shimmering tails. Blend them, or feed one into the
other.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/chamber/card" alt="Nota Chamber, Convolution tab with the impulse response" />

**Layout:** four views in the centre — **Convolution** (the IR as a clip, **Predelay**, **Size**
and **Attack** beside it, the IR picker under it), **Algorithm** (the decay time by frequency,
**Decay**, **Size** and **Damping** beside it, the mode under it), **EQ · Mod** (the tail EQ, its
cuts beside it, its position under it) and **Mix** (the signal flow, **Blend**, **Width** and
**Mono f** beside it, parallel / serial under it) — and a right panel that follows the tab: the
convolution engine (on, true stereo, reverse, sync, quality), the algorithm (on, Freeze, Diffuse,
Predelay, Hold in, Vintage, Sync), **Mod** (rate, depth, the shimmer) and **Mix** (ducking, wet
only, zero latency). Under it on every tab sit **Dry**, **Dry/Wet** and **Output**. The **S / L**
switch in the header folds the card to **S**: the IR over its picker and **Predelay**, **Size**,
**Attack** and **Dry/Wet**.

## Convolution

Sixteen built-in impulse responses — halls (**Concert Hall · Wide**, **Stone Vault**, **Cathedral**,
**Scoring Stage**), rooms (**Wood Chamber**, **Live Room**, **Drum Room**, **Tiled Bathroom**), plates
(**Vocal Plate**, **Bright Plate**), **Spring Tank**, spaces (**Car Park**, **Stairwell**, **Forest
Clearing**) and FX (**Gated Room**, **Metal Tank**) — or **your own**: drop a WAV, FLAC or MP3 on the
IR view, or use **Load…** (mono, stereo or 4-channel true stereo).

The view shows the recording like an audio clip. Drag the **Start** and **End** brackets to keep part
of it; inside them the waveform is drawn as you hear it — faded in by **Attack**, faded out at a
trimmed end, backwards with **Reverse** — and a dashed line marks where the fades act. **Size**
time-stretches it, **True Stereo** uses all four channels of a 4-channel IR, and the pre-delay runs
free or synced. The engine has
zero latency; turning **Zero Latency** off saves CPU.

## Algorithm

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/chamber/algorithm" alt="Nota Chamber, Algorithm tab" />

**Dark Hall**, **Plate**, **Quartz** or **Shimmer**. The graph shows how long each part of the
spectrum rings: the **Lows**, **Mids** and **Highs** plateaus, each with a handle — drag it up for a
longer decay (the mids are **Decay**, and the lows and highs follow them as ratios) — and the dashed
**Damping** line where the highs begin, which you drag sideways. Then **Size**, **Diffuse**, **Damping**, modulation, a **Vintage** colour, and for Shimmer a
pitch shifter (fed back into the tail, or on top). **Freeze** holds the tail (input muted unless
**Freeze In** is on). Its own pre-delay, free or synced.

## EQ · Mod

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/chamber/eq-mod" alt="Nota Chamber, EQ · Mod tab" />

A 4-band EQ you can place **at the input**, **on the tail** or **at the output** — drag its handles —
and the modulation settings.

## Mix

The **Mix** tab draws the signal flow: the input splits into the dry path and the two engines — side
by side (**Parallel**) or the convolution feeding the algorithm (**Serial**) — whose **Blend** runs
through the EQ (wherever it sits), the stereo stage (**Width**, bass mono below **Mono f**) and
**Ducking** (the input pushes the reverb down while it plays), meets the dry at the mix and leaves at
the output. Click an engine to turn it on or off, or the EQ to move it. **Wet only** is for a
return.

## Presets

22 factory presets: halls (**Stone Vault**, **Concert Hall**, **Cathedral Wash**, **Scoring Stage**,
**Vintage Hall**, **Ducked Vocal Hall**), rooms (**Wood Chamber**, **Drum Room**, **Live Room**, **Car
Park**, **Gated Snare**), plates and springs (**Vocal Plate**, **Snare Plate**, **Spring Box**, **Metal
Tank**), effects (**Wide Shimmer**, **Octave Down Bloom**, **Endless Pad**, **Reverse Swell**, **Forest
Echoes**) and for returns (**Send · Big Hall**, **Send · Plate**).
