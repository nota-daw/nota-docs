Nota Vintage makes things sound old, warm or worn: a dusty record, a stretched cassette, a VHS
soundtrack, a tube preamp, a console bus.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/vintage/card" alt="Nota Vintage, Curve tab with the transfer curve" />

**Signal flow:** wow / flutter → saturation → output stage → tone, head bump and band limit → hiss
and crackle → auto gain → mix.

**Layout:** the **State** column on the left (**Drive** and **Wear** faders), a centre panel with
three tabs headed by the character list, a right panel and a status line.

## The eras

Each **character** sets its own saturation, tone tilt, head bump, band limit and baseline hiss,
crackle and wow: **Vinyl**, **Cassette**, **Reel-to-Reel**, **VHS**, **Tube**, **Analog**. **Character
off** keeps your settings without the era's voicing.

## Curve

The transfer curve the saturation runs — **Drive** pushes it harder. **Even harmonics only** gives
a warm, tube-like colour without grit.

## Wear

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/vintage/wear" alt="Nota Vintage, Wear tab with the pitch drift" />

The pitch drift over the last four seconds. **Wow** (slow, free or synced to the tempo) and
**Flutter** (fast), each with its rate; **Wear** — an age macro that raises everything at once, and
can follow the input; **Stereo drift** lets the channels wander apart.

## Output

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/vintage/output" alt="Nota Vintage, Output tab with the harmonics and the hiss floor" />

The harmonics against the hiss floor. **Noise** (hiss, with a **Hiss HP** filter) and **Crackle**;
tone — **Tone**, a **Warm**, **Flat** or **Dark** model, **Low** and **High** shelves; the output
stage (**Tube** or **Analog**), **Oversampling**, **Auto comp**, **Mix** and **Output**.

## Presets

30 factory presets: vinyl (**Dusty Vinyl**, **78 RPM Shellac**, **Clean Crackle**, **Warped Record**,
**Gated Dust**), tape (**Lo-Fi Beat Tape**, **Warped Cassette**, **Worn Deck**, **Chewed Tape**,
**Walkman**, **Reel Warmth**, **Tape Saturation**, **Mastering Tape**, **Tape Sway 1/2**, **Seasick 2
Bars**), video (**VHS Fever**, **VHS Dream**, **Broken Tracking**), tube and analog (**Tube Glow**,
**Tube Glue**, **Even Warmth**, **Hot Preamp**, **Parallel Tube**, **Analog Glue**, **Console Bus**,
**Transformer Color**, **Crunch Drive**, **Neutral Saturator**) and radio (**Telephone Line**, **AM
Radio**).
