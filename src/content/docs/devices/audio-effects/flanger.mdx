Nota Flanger mixes a sound with a copy of itself delayed by a fraction of a millisecond to a few
milliseconds. The delay moves, and a comb of notches sweeps up and down: the jet-plane swoosh.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/flanger/card" alt="Nota Flanger: the LFO, the live comb response and the delay line" />

## LFO

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Wave | Sine, triangle or saw |
| **Hz** / **Sync** | Free, 0.02–8 Hz, or synced from 2 bars to 1/16 (with dotted and triplets) |
| **Rate** | The speed, with its period |
| **Stereo** | The right channel's LFO runs 0–180° behind the left — wider as it grows |

## The window

The live comb response of L and R — drag a notch left and right for **Delay**, up and down for
**Feedback** — and under it the delay of both channels over two cycles.

## Delay line

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Delay** | Base delay, 0.1–8 ms — where the comb sits |
| **Depth** | How far the LFO sweeps it |
| **Feedback** | Bipolar, with − / 0 / + snaps. **Positive** sharpens the peaks — the classic jet; **negative** mirrors the comb — hollow and tube-like. Near ±95 % it rings metallically |
| **Mix** | 0 dry · 50 % the deepest notches · 100 % wet only, a pure vibrato |

The column shows the first notch's sweep and null depth; the status line names the mode.

Air**, **Pad Space**), hollow (**Hollow Bar**, **Hollow Tube**, **Inside Out**, **Half-Bar Hollow**),
metallic (**Tin Robot**, **Metal Ring**, **Laser Zap**, **Comb Drone**, **Siren**), synced (**Quarter
Swirl**, **Two-Bar Sweep**, **Eighth Pulse**, **Dotted Drift**, **Triplet Shimmer**) and more
