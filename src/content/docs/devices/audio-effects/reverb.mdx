Nota Reverb puts a sound in a space — from a tight room to a cathedral, from a bright plate to an
endless frozen wash. It's the reverb to reach for first; for convolution and hybrid spaces see
[Nota Chamber](../chamber/).

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/reverb/card" alt="Nota Reverb, Space tab with the live tail against the set decay" />

**Layout:** the **Tail** column on the left (**Decay**, **HF Damp**), a centre panel with two tabs,
each one live graph of your audio that fills it, a right panel with the controls of the tab you're
on (or **Output**), and a status line. Both graphs are controls too.

| Centre tab | Right panel |
|---|---|
| **Space** | Mode, Pre, Size, Diffuse, Decay, Early reflections, Freeze |
| **Tone · Mod** | Low cut, High cut, HF Damp, Mod rate, Depth, Mod on tail, Vintage |

## Space

The last six seconds of your audio: the input as a grey wash, the reverb's tail as a line, and the
dashed line is the decay you've set, falling from the latest hit. When the tail follows the dashed
line, the reverb rings as long as **Decay** says. **Drag the graph** to set the decay (right or up is
longer), double-click to reset it. Right of the tabs: the mode, the RT60 and the pre-delay.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mode** | **Hall**, **Room**, **Plate** or **Chamber** |
| **Pre** | Pre-delay — the gap before the reverb starts |
| **Size** | The size of the space |
| **Diffuse** | How dense the reflections get |
| **Early reflections** | The first, distinct echoes of the room |
| **Decay** | Reverb time, as a true RT60 |
| **HF Damp** | How fast the highs die away |

## Tone · Mod

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/reverb/tone-mod" alt="Nota Reverb, Tone · Mod tab" />

How long the tail rings at each frequency (seconds, up the left): raise **HF Damp** and the highs die
sooner. The shaded edges are the **Low cut** and **High cut** on the tail; drag their handles, double-click
to reset. The grey line underneath is the tail's live spectrum. Right of the tabs: the decay at 250 Hz
and at 4 kHz.

**Mod rate** and **Depth** add a chorus-like shimmer — on the
whole reverb, or only the tail (**Mod on tail**). **Vintage** gives it the band-limited, grainy colour of
early digital reverbs.

## Output

**Dry**, **Mix** (dry / wet), **Width**, **Mono** (bass mono below a frequency), **Wet only** (for a
return track), **Out**, and **Latency comp.** — starts the tail exactly where the pre-delay says.
**Kill tail** clears what's ringing; the **Wet** meter shows the tail's level. **Freeze** (on the **Space**
panel) holds the tail forever, and the input is muted.

## Presets

30 factory presets: rooms (**Small Room**, **Tight Room**, **Drum Room**, **Vocal Booth**, **Live
Room**), halls (**Wide Hall**, **Long Hall**, **Concert Hall**, **Cathedral**, **Dark Hall**), plates
(**Bright Plate**, **Vocal Plate**, **Snare Plate**, **Gold Plate**, **Vintage Plate**), chambers
(**Echo Chamber**, **Studio Chamber**, **Stone Chamber**), ambient and frozen (**Shimmer Freeze**,
**Stone Hold**, **Infinite Pad**, **Ambient Wash**, **Still Water**, **Lo-Fi Cloud**, **Seasick**),
short (**Short Burst**, **Slap Space**) and for returns (**Send · Hall**, **Send · Plate**, **Send ·
Room**).

## Tips

- **On a return:** put Reverb on a return track with **Wet only** on, and send each track to it.
- **Keep the low end clean:** raise **Low cut** so the reverb doesn't muddy the bass.
