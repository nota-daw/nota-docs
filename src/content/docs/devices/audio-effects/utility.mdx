Nota Utility does the plain but essential jobs: make it wider or narrower, mono the bass, flip
polarity, trim the level — or hold it at a loudness target.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/utility/card" alt="Nota Utility, Field tab with the stereo field by frequency" />

**Layout:** the **Level** column on the left (input and output meters), a centre panel with three
tabs, a right panel (**Routing** / **Output**) and a status line.

## Field

The stereo field by frequency — where each band sits, how wide, how correlated.

## Mono

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/utility/mono" alt="Nota Utility, Mono tab" />

The width over frequency with the mono region marked. **Mono below** a frequency (**Mono f**), at 6,
12 or 24 dB/oct, keeps the low end centred.

## Levels

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/utility/levels" alt="Nota Utility, Levels tab" />

Input and output over the last 8 seconds against the target, in the chosen **Meter** unit:
**LUFS-S**, sample peak or RMS.

## Routing and Output

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Channel** | **Stereo**, **Left**, **Right** or **Swap** |
| **Width** | Stereo width; two laws — **L/R** (the side is scaled; wider gets louder) or **M/S** (mid and side are traded; 200 % = side only) |
| **Balance** | Left / right balance |
| **Invert L** · **Invert R** | Flip a channel's polarity |
| **Mute** | Mute the output |
| **Gain** | Output gain |
| **Gain match** | A one-shot match: sets Gain so the output equals the input (or the target) |
| **Level match** | Continuous auto-match, to the input's level or to a **Target** |
| **Ceiling** | A true-peak safety limiter at the end, with its ceiling |

## Presets

31 factory presets: width (**Stereo Widener**, **Wide**, **Wide Master**, **Wide Pad**, **Narrow**,
**Mono**, **Side Only**), low end (**Bass Mono**, **Mono Maker**, **Club Low End**, **Sub Mono 60**,
**Vinyl Safe**), routing and polarity (**Swap L/R**, **Left Only**, **Right Only**, **Polarity Flip**,
**Fix Left Polarity**, **Mute**), gain (**Trim −6 dB**, **Boost +6 dB**, **Gain Match −9**) and
loudness (**Level Held**, **Level Match**, **RMS Match**, **Streaming −14 LUFS**, **Podcast −16
LUFS**, **Broadcast −23 LUFS**, **Peak Target −3 dB**, **True-Peak Safety**).

## Tips

- **Fair A/B:** put Utility after a chain and use **Gain match** to hear the effect, not the
  loudness change.
- **For streaming:** for a final loudness tool with a gain ride, see [Nota Level](../level/).
