Nota EQ-3 is a **performance** EQ, the kind on a DJ mixer: three bands, a fader and a **Kill** each.
Pull the bass out before a drop and slam it back in, thin a breakdown, swap the low end between
two tracks.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/eq-3/card" alt="Nota EQ-3 with three band strips and the response window" />

## Controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Low** · **Mid** · **High** | A fader per band: −24 … +6 dB in the **Isolator** range, ±15 dB in **Classic** |
| **Kill** | Removes the band completely — with a short fade, so it never clicks |
| **Crossover** | The two split points (Low / Mid and Mid / High); drag the handles along the top of the graph |
| **Slope** | **LR 24** or **LR 48** — how steep the split is |
| **Range** | **Isolator** (the default) or **Classic** |
| **Out** | Output level |

At 0 dB on every band the three bands add back up flat — the split is phase-aligned. The window
shows each band's curve in its colour, the sum in brass and the output spectrum behind.

## Presets

30 factory presets: starts (**Classic Flat**, **Isolator 48**), kills and isolations (**DJ Kill
Bass**, **Kill Mids**, **Kill Highs**, **Bass Swap**, **Bass Only**, **Mids Only**, **Highs Only**),
transitions (**Transition Build**, **Breakdown Thin**), curves (**Scoop**, **Smile**, **Bass Boost**,
**Warm Up**, **Air Lift**, **Presence**), focus (**Sub Tighten**, **Mud Cut**, **Vocal Focus**,
**Kick Focus**, **Hat Sizzle**, **Club Master**) and colour (**Telephone**, **AM Radio**, **Lo-Fi
Dull**, **Megaphone**).

## Tips

- **Automate the kills:** draw a Low Kill for the last bar before a drop.
- **Map the faders** to a controller with MIDI Learn and play it like a DJ mixer.
