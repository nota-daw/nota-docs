Nota Prism splits the sound into **Low**, **Mid** and **High** and treats each band's dynamics on its
own: tame the low end without touching the vocal, lift the quiet details of the highs, glue a bus,
squash everything upward.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/prism/card" alt="Nota Prism, Bands tab with the spectrum and crossovers" />

**Layout:** three tabs in the centre — **Bands** (the spectrum with its crossovers over a row per
band), **Band detail** (the selected band's transfer curve and its above / below knobs; the band
chips sit in the tab bar) and **Time** (the detector trace over each band's attack and release) —
and a right panel that follows the tab: **Gain reduction** (the band count, the meters, Solo, Peak
hold), the output stage (Auto makeup, the selected band's GR, boost and level, Soft clip) and
**Detector** (Peak / RMS, Lookahead, the sidechain, Listen). Under it on every tab sit **Amount**,
**Dry/Wet** and **Output**. The **S / L** switch in the header folds the card to **S**: the spectrum
over the band count and **Amount**, **Dry/Wet** and **Output**.

Bands are colour-coded: **Low** olive, **Mid** neutral ink, **High** teal.

## Bands

The spectrum with the two **crossovers** — drag them. Choose 3 bands, 2 (Low + Mid) or 1 (full band).
The crossovers are phase-aligned, so the bands add back up flat.

## Band detail

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/prism/band-detail" alt="Nota Prism, Band detail tab with the transfer curve" />

Each band has two processors — drag their threshold dots on the transfer curve:

| Processor | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Above** | Compresses what goes over its threshold — ratio 1:1 to ∞:1, soft knee |
| **Below** | Under a second threshold: expands downward (ratio > 1) or lifts upward (ratio < 1), within ± **Floor** dB |

Each with **Attack** and **Release** (auto release on Above), and a band **Gain**. **Solo** a band to
hear it alone.

## Time

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/prism/time" alt="Nota Prism, Time tab" />

Each band's input and detector level over time, so you see what the dynamics react to.

## Global and Output

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Amount** | Scales every ratio — 0 % switches the dynamics off |
| **Detect** | Peak or RMS |
| **Lookahead** | Reacts before peaks; compensated automatically |
| **Auto makeup** | Restores the level lost to compression |
| **Mix** | Parallel blend with the dry signal, phase-coherent |
| **Output** · soft clip | Output gain, and a soft clipper at −0.3 dBFS |

A sidechain from another track is split into the same bands, so each band keys off the matching band
of the key; **Listen** lets you hear the key.

## Presets

12 factory presets: **Bus Glue**, **Upward Squash**, **Noise Floor Cleanup**, **Single-Band Leveler**,
**Parallel Crush**, **Low-End Tamer**, **Gentle Master**, **Air Lift**, **Vocal Control**, **De-Esser**,
**Drum Punch** and **Two-Band Bass**.
