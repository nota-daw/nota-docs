Nota Auto Shift corrects pitch as it plays — gently, so a vocal just sits in tune, or hard, for the
robotic hard-tune sound. It can also follow a **key it learns** from the singing, or the **notes of
another track**.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/auto-shift/card" alt="Nota Auto Shift, Trace tab with detected and corrected pitch" />

**How it works:** it **detects** the voice's pitch, picks a **target** note, and **shifts** the voice
there while keeping the formants (the voice's character) where they were. The dry signal is delayed
by the same amount, and the delay is compensated, so **Mix** never smears.

**Layout:** the **Pitch** column on the left (where the voice is, the correction now), a centre panel
with three tabs, a right panel (**Shift** / **Detect**) and a status line.

## Trace

The detected pitch and the corrected pitch on the scale's note lanes, over time.

## Scale

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/auto-shift/scale" alt="Nota Auto Shift, Scale tab with the sung-note histogram" />

**Key** and **Scale** — **Chromatic**, **Major**, **Minor**, **Penta Maj**, **Penta Min** — or a
**Custom** set: click notes on and off. A histogram of the notes actually sung sits under them.
**Learn** listens for about 16 bars and picks the key; **Key Source** **Auto** follows the learned
key. **Follow Scale device** copies the key and scale of a [Nota Scale](../../midi-effects/scale/)
on the track.

## MIDI

The target comes from the notes another track plays (pick it as the device's sidechain source):
**Note** mode snaps to the exact note, **Scale** mode uses the notes as a scale; **Latch**, **Oct
Lock** and a **Bend** range shape it.

## Shift

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Amount** | How far the pitch is pulled to the target |
| **Speed** | How fast it gets there — slow is natural, instant is hard tune |
| **Human** | Keeps vibrato around the corrected note |
| **Range** | Only corrects notes within this distance |
| **Shift** · **Fine** | Transpose the result |
| **Formant** · **Formant shift** | Keep the voice's character, or move it on its own (bigger, smaller voice) |
| **Mix** | Dry / wet |

## Detect

**Det Low** / **Det High** — the note range to look for — and **Det Sens**; **Skip sibilants** leaves
"s" and "t" sounds alone.

## Presets

34 factory presets: correction (**Hard Tune**, **Robot Voice**, **Natural Vocal**, **Transparent
Touch**, **Pop Lead**, **R&B Runs**, **Ballad**, **Rap Hook**, **Choir Tighten**), keys (**Auto Key**,
**Chromatic Fix**, **Minor Key**, **Pentatonic Pop**, **Blues in A**, **Dorian Groove**), voices
(**Soprano**, **Alto**, **Tenor**, **Bass Voice**, **Lead Instrument**), effects (**Octave Up**, **Octave
Down**, **Chipmunk**, **Monster**, **Gender Up**, **Gender Down**, **Formant Up**, **Detune Double**,
**Fifth Harmony**, **Harmony Lock**) and MIDI (**MIDI Hard Tune**, **MIDI Scale Follow**, **MIDI Melody
Replace**).
