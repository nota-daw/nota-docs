Nota Auto Shift corrects pitch as it plays — gently, so a vocal just sits in tune, or hard, for the
robotic hard-tune sound. It can also follow a **key it learns** from the singing, or the **notes of
another track**.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/auto-shift/card" alt="Nota Auto Shift, Trace tab with detected and corrected pitch" />

**How it works:** it **detects** the voice's pitch, picks a **target** note, and **shifts** the voice
there while keeping the formants (the voice's character) where they were. The dry signal is delayed
by the same amount, and the delay is compensated, so **Mix** never smears.

**Layout:** a centre panel with three tabs — **Trace**, **Scale**, **MIDI**, each a window with its
own knobs beside it — and a right panel that follows the tab: **Detect** on Trace, **Key** on
Scale, **MIDI** on MIDI. Under the right panel, whichever tab is open, sit **Preserve formants** and
the output knobs **Shift**, **Dry/Wet** and **Formant**. The tab bar reads out what the voice is
doing now (input note → target). The **S / L** switch in the header folds the card to **S**: the
trace over **Key**, **Scale**, **Amount**, **Speed**, **Human** and **Dry/Wet**.

## Trace

The detected pitch and the corrected pitch on the scale's note lanes, over time, with the knobs
that shape the correction beside it:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Amount** | How far the pitch is pulled to the target |
| **Speed** | How fast it gets there — slow is natural, instant is hard tune |
| **Human** | Keeps vibrato around the corrected note |

The right panel is **Detect**: a voice preset in its title bar (**Voice · soprano**, **Instrument**…)
sets **Low** / **High** — the note range to look for — and **Sens**; **Skip sibilants** leaves "s"
and "t" sounds alone.

## Scale

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/auto-shift/scale" alt="Nota Auto Shift, Scale tab with the sung-note histogram" />

A histogram of the notes actually sung, with the scale's notes in a row under it: click a note
to add it or take it out (the scale becomes **Custom**).

The right panel is **Key**. Its title bar picks where the key comes from: **Auto** follows the key
you sing, **Manual** uses the one you set, **MIDI** takes the target from another track (the MIDI
tab). Under it: **Key** and **Scale** (**Chromatic**, **Major**, **Minor**, **Penta Maj**, **Penta
Min**, eight more modes, or **Custom**), **Fine** (±100 cents), **Range** (only notes within this
distance are corrected), **Learn** — listens for about 16 bars and shows the key it found — and
**Follow Scale device**, which copies the key and scale of a [Nota Scale](../../midi-effects/scale/)
on the track.

## MIDI

The target comes from the notes another track plays. **Glide** and **Bend** (the widest move that
glides; wider leaps jump) sit beside the window. The right panel is **MIDI**: the source track in
its title bar, **Note** (snap to the exact note) or **Scale** (use the notes as a scale), **Latch**,
**Oct lock** and **Pitch bend from MIDI**.

## Output

**Preserve formants** keeps the voice's character when it is shifted. **Shift** transposes the
result (±12 semitones), **Dry/Wet** blends, **Formant** moves the formants on their own (a bigger or
smaller voice).

## Presets

34 factory presets: correction (**Hard Tune**, **Robot Voice**, **Natural Vocal**, **Transparent
Touch**, **Pop Lead**, **R&B Runs**, **Ballad**, **Rap Hook**, **Choir Tighten**), keys (**Auto Key**,
**Chromatic Fix**, **Minor Key**, **Pentatonic Pop**, **Blues in A**, **Dorian Groove**), voices
(**Soprano**, **Alto**, **Tenor**, **Bass Voice**, **Lead Instrument**), effects (**Octave Up**, **Octave
Down**, **Chipmunk**, **Monster**, **Gender Up**, **Gender Down**, **Formant Up**, **Detune Double**,
**Fifth Harmony**, **Harmony Lock**) and MIDI (**MIDI Hard Tune**, **MIDI Scale Follow**, **MIDI Melody
Replace**).
