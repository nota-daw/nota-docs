Nota Orbit moves a sound in volume and in space with one LFO. The **Phase** between the left and
right channels decides what you hear: at **0°** both dip together — tremolo; at **180°** one is loud
while the other is quiet — auto-pan; in between, a swirl.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/orbit/card" alt="Nota Orbit: the LFO, both channels' gain over two cycles and the motion controls" />

## LFO

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Wave | **Sine**, **Triangle**, **Saw**, **Square** or **S&H** (random steps) |
| **Hz** / **Sync** | Free, 0.01–40 Hz, or synced to a division from 4 bars to 1/32, including dotted and triplets |
| **Rate** | The speed (shown big, with its period) |
| **Shape** | Sharpens the smooth waves toward a square; on S&H it's the **Glide** between steps |

Synced, the LFO locks to the song position and restarts on the bar.

## The window

Both channels' gain over two cycles, with a running head and the depth's floor. Drag up and down
for **Amount**, left and right for **Phase**. Underneath, the stereo position on an L–R line.

## Motion

**Amount** (depth), **Phase** with snaps at **0°**, **90°** and **180°**, **Mix** (dry / wet) and the
output meters. The status line names the mode it makes — tremolo, auto-pan or an offset in
between.
