Nota Compressor evens out a signal: it turns down what goes over the threshold, then makes the
result louder again. Glue a drum bus, level a vocal, pump a pad from the kick.

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/compressor/card" alt="Nota Compressor, Curve tab with the transfer curve and reduction history" />

**Layout:** the **Level** column on the left (threshold against the live level, make-up), a centre
panel with three tabs, a right panel (**Dynamics** / **Output**) and a status line.

## Curve

The transfer curve and the history of gain reduction. **Drag the curve** to set threshold and ratio.

## Motion

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/compressor/motion" alt="Nota Compressor, Motion tab" />

The gain-reduction envelope drawn against the input, so you **see** attack and release work
rather than guess.

## Sidechain

<Shot id="devices/audio-effects/compressor/sidechain" alt="Nota Compressor, Sidechain tab with the key filters" />

What the detector listens to: **Internal** (the track) or **External key** (another track, picked
as the device's sidechain source, **Pre** or **Post** its fader). The key's spectrum is drawn under
its filters — drag the **HP** and **LP** handles; **Q** shapes both. **Listen** lets you hear the key.

## Dynamics and Output

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Thresh** · **Ratio** · **Knee** | Where it starts, how hard, how gradually |
| **Attack** · **Hold** · **Release** | How fast it clamps, holds, lets go — **Auto** release follows the material |
| **Detect** | **Peak**, **RMS** or **Auto** |
| **Lookahead** | Reacts before the peak arrives; the delay is compensated automatically |
| **Range** | The most it will ever reduce |
| **Char** | Five voicings: **Clean**, **Glue**, **Punch**, **Opto**, **FET** |
| **Makeup** · **Auto gain** | Gain after compression, by hand or automatic |
| **Mix** | Dry / wet — parallel compression in one knob |
| **Stereo link** | One gain for both channels, or each channel on its own |

## Presets

31 factory presets: drums (**Drum Glue**, **Drum Punch**, **Snare Punch**, **Kick Tighten**, **Room
Smash**, **Overheads Tame**, **Hat Smooth**), bus and master (**Bus Glue**, **Bus Parallel**, **Mix
Bus Gentle**, **Master Leveler**, **Brick Limit**, **Peak Catcher**), vocals (**Vocal Opto**, **Vocal
Leveler**, **Vocal Presence**, **Rap Vocal**, **De-Esser**), instruments (**Bass Even**, **Bass
Pluck**, **Acoustic Guitar**, **Guitar Sustain**, **Piano Smooth**, **Pad Breathe**) and ducking
(**Kick Duck**, **EDM Pump**, **Gentle Duck**, **Voice-over Duck**, **Bass Under Kick**).

## Tips

- **Sidechain pump:** External key from the kick, fast attack, release to taste — or start from
  **EDM Pump**.
- **Parallel drums:** a heavy ratio with **Mix** around 40 %.
