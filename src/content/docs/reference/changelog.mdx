Nota gets a new release every week or so. Each one lists its changes three ways:

- **In the app** — after an update, **What's New** shows everything that changed since your version;
  open it any time from **Help ▸ What's New**.
- **On GitHub** — every release, with its notes and downloads.
- **In the source** — the full history in `CHANGELOG.md`.
