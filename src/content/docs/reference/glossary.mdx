**Arm** — make a track ready to record (the **●** in its header).

**Arrangement** — the view that lays the song out on a timeline. See [Arrangement](../../views/arrangement/).

**Automation** — a value changing over time, drawn or recorded in a lane. See [Automation](../../editing/automation/).

**Audio track** — a track that holds audio clips and has no instrument.

**AU / VST3** — the plug-in formats Nota hosts: VST3 everywhere, AU on macOS. See [Plug-ins](../../plugins/).

**Chain** — the devices on a track, or one strand inside a rack, processed left to right.

**Choke group** — pads that cut each other off, like an open and a closed hi-hat.

**Clip** — a block of notes (MIDI clip) or audio (audio clip) on a track or in a Session slot.

**Consolidate** — join the material in a range into one clip per track.

**Count-in** — bars of clicks before playback or recording starts.

**CV** — a control signal in the Modular view that moves a parameter. See [Modular](../../views/modular/).

**Detail panel** — the panel under the view that shows devices, a step pattern or a clip.

**Device** — an instrument, audio effect or MIDI effect. See [Devices](../../devices/).

**Follow action** — what a Session clip or scene does after it has played for a while.

**Freeze / Flatten** — render a track to audio to save CPU; flatten makes that permanent.

**Group** — a track that holds other tracks and acts as their submix.

**Kit** — a set of drum sounds loaded on a Drum Rack or Nota Rhythm.

**Launch quantize** — the grid Session clips and scenes start on (Quant).

**Legato** — playing a new note before releasing the last; synths can slide instead of retriggering.

**Loop / one-shot** — a sample that repeats in time, versus a single hit.

**LUFS** — perceived loudness, as streaming services measure it.

**Macro** — a knob on a rack or kit that drives many parameters at once. See [Macros](../../devices/racks/macros/).

**Master** — the sum of all tracks, going to your speakers.

**MCP** — the protocol an AI assistant uses to work in Nota. See [AI control over MCP](../../ai/mcp/).

**MIDI Learn** — mapping a hardware or phone control to a control in Nota. See [MIDI Learn](../../control/midi-learn/).

**Modulator** — an LFO, envelope or similar source that moves parameters over time.

**Monitor** — hear a track's input live through its effects.

**PDC** — plug-in delay compensation: Nota delays the other tracks so everything stays in time.

**Preset** — saved settings for a device. Factory presets ship with Nota; you can save your own.

**Project key** — the key set in the transport bar; loops can be transposed to it.

**Quantize** — snap notes to the grid.

**Rack** — a device that holds several chains: Instrument Rack, Drum Rack, Audio Effect Rack.

**Return track** — a shared effect bus that tracks feed through their **sends**.

**Scene** — a row in the Session view, launched as one.

**Section** — a named stretch of the song in the Arrangement's Sections lane.

**Session** — the clip-launching view. See [Session](../../views/session/).

**Sidechain** — feeding one track's signal to a device on another track to control it.

**Slot** — one cell of the Session grid.

**Stem** — one part of a mix on its own (drums, bass, vocals…), from export or Separate Stems.

**Time range** — a stretch of time across tracks, selected with ⇧-drag.

**True peak** — the highest level between samples, which a converter can actually reach.

**Unison** — several detuned copies of each voice, for width.

**Velocity** — how hard a note is played.

**Version** — a saved state of the project in its history. See [Version history](../../basics/version-history/).

**Warp** — stretching audio to the project tempo without changing its pitch.

**Zone** — the key or velocity range a rack chain answers to.
