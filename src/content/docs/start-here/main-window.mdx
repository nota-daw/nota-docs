Everything in Nota happens in one window. It has four areas: the **transport bar** along the
top, the **browser** on the left, the **view** in the middle, and the **detail panel**
underneath, which opens when you select a track or a clip.

<Shot id="start-here/main-window-devices" alt="The Nota main window: transport bar on top, browser on the left, the Arrangement in the middle and the devices of the Keys track below" />

## Transport bar

<Shot id="basics/transport-bar" alt="The transport bar" />

From left to right:

- **Arrangement · Session · Modular** — switch the view in the middle.
- **Stop, Play, Record** and the **position** — click the position to switch between bars
  and minutes:seconds.
- **LOOP** — the loop range set on the ruler.
- **BPM**, **SIG** (time signature), **KEY** (project key) and **GRID** (the snap grid).
- **Metronome**, **follow playhead**, **snap to grid** and **automation lanes**.
- **MIDI** — map a hardware controller to any control ([MIDI Learn](../../control/midi-learn/)).
- **Remote** — play and control Nota from your phone ([Nota Remote](../../control/nota-remote/)).
- **MST** — the master volume and meter.

Every control is explained in [Transport](../../basics/transport/).

## Browser

The browser holds everything you can bring into a project. The icons down its left edge are
its tabs:

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Instruments** | Nota's instruments and your plug-in instruments, with their presets |
| **Audio effects** | Built-in effects and plug-in effects |
| **MIDI effects** | Arpeggiator, chords, scales and other note processors |
| **Files** | Your sample library and any folder you add, with smart tags |
| **Presets** | The presets you saved yourself, by category and device (factory presets sit under each device in the first three tabs) |
| **Projects** | The projects in your projects folder |
| **MIDI map** | Every control you've mapped to a controller or phone |
| **History** | The [versions](../../basics/version-history/) of the open project |

Double-click an instrument to add a track with it; double-click an effect to add it to the
selected track; or drag either where you want it. The button at the bottom of the rail
collapses the browser. More in [The browser](../../library/browser/).

## The view

The middle of the window shows one of three views:

- **[Arrangement](../../views/arrangement/)** — the song on a timeline, left to right.
- **[Session](../../views/session/)** — a grid of clips you launch live, scene by scene.
- **[Modular](../../views/modular/)** — a track's devices as a graph you can patch.

The **Mixer** opens in a window of its own: **View ▸ Mixer** (<Kbd>⌘ ⇧ M</Kbd>).

## Detail panel

Select a track and its devices appear in the panel at the bottom; double-click a clip and it
opens there for editing. The tabs at its top left switch between **Devices**, **Pattern**
(the step pattern of a Drum Rack clip) and **Clip** — <Kbd>Tab</Kbd> cycles them. On the
right, **Freeze** and **Live Freeze** render a track to audio to save CPU, the ⧉ button moves
the panel into a [window of its own](../../views/detail-panel/), and × closes it.

## Menus and the status line

- **File** — new, open, save, versions, import and export.
- **Edit** — undo, clipboard, splitting, consolidating, duplicating time.
- **View** — the mixer, Connect Phone…, and toggles for the browser, the detail panel, the
  overview, the sections lane, following the playhead and clip names.
- **Transport** — play, record, loop and metronome.
- **Help** — Documentation (this manual), What's New and About.

The line along the bottom of the window reports what just happened ("Saved Night Drive",
"Added Nota Volt") and shows the audio sample rate.

<Aside title="⌘ or Ctrl?">
Shortcuts are written the macOS way. On Windows and Linux, <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> is <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>
and <Kbd>⌥</Kbd> is <Kbd>Alt</Kbd>. All of them are listed in
[Keyboard shortcuts](../../reference/shortcuts/).
</Aside>
