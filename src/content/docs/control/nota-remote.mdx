Nota Remote turns a phone or tablet into a controller. Nothing to install: Nota serves the page itself,
over your local Wi-Fi or a USB cable.

## Connecting

<Steps>

1. Click **Remote** in the transport bar (or **View ▸ Connect Phone…**). The first time, allow
   control from a phone — on macOS, also allow Nota to find devices on the local network.

2. Scan the QR code with the phone's camera. Or open the address shown and type the four-digit code
   (it changes every two minutes).

3. The phone plays Nota within seconds. Add it to the Home Screen for a full-screen app that
   reconnects by itself after a locked screen or a dropped link.

</Steps>

<Shot id="control/nota-remote/connect-phone" alt="The Connect Phone panel with the QR code, the address, the code and connected phones" width={360} />

A phone you've paired is **trusted**: it reconnects from its Home Screen icon without a code until you
forget it in [Settings ▸ Remote](../../preferences/remote/).

**USB** works too — a phone on a cable (USB tethering on Android, Internet Sharing on the Mac) gets an
instant, radio-free link. Nota notices the cable, puts its address in the QR, and marks the phone
**USB**.

## The screens

Every screen shares one header: the track picker, a mini transport and the link status. Tap the
position for the **big transport** (play, stop, record, loop, metronome, undo, tap tempo, jump to
sections).

| Screen | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Pads** | A grid of pads — a Drum Rack's pads by name, banks, full velocity, note repeat |
| **Keys** | A **Keyboard**, a **Scale** grid in the project key, or **Chords**; **Expressive** for MPE synths |
| **XY** | An XY pad on the track's controls, with **Hold** / **Return** and **Tilt** — the phone's motion drives it |
| **Mixer** | Faders, mute, solo and arm for every track |
| **Macros** | The selected device's macros as knobs |
| **Scenes** | The Session grid — launch clips and scenes |

The screens read like Nota's own: orange marks what is playing, held or switched on; **M** lights
amber, **S** blue and **Arm** red, as in the Mixer.

On a track whose synth plays [MPE](../midi/#pitch-wheel-aftertouch-and-mpe), **Keys** shows an
**Expressive** switch in Keyboard and Scale. With it on, a finger keeps the note it struck instead of
gliding to the next key: slide sideways to bend it (a key's width is its pitch step — a white key on
the keyboard, a scale step on the grid), and up or down the key to change its timbre. A phone or tablet
that senses touch force also sends pressure.

## Colours

The phone wears Nota's colour theme and changes with it. Pick another theme in
[Settings ▸ Appearance](../../preferences/appearance/), or let **Follow system** switch Nota between
dark and light, and the phone follows within a moment — no reload, and a held note keeps sounding.

You choose how in the phone's **⋯** menu, under **Theme**:

| Choice | What the phone wears |
|---|---|
| **Follow Nota** | The theme Nota wears right now (the default) |
| **Follow phone** | Your **In dark mode** theme while the phone is in dark mode, your **In light mode** theme while it is light — the two picks in Settings ▸ Appearance |

The line under the switch says what the phone is wearing and why — for example *Studio Dark · as Nota
wears it*.

starts in Studio Dark until Nota sends its colours.

## A phone is one more controller

Its notes record like the computer keyboard's; faders and macros move Nota's controls (one gesture,
one undo step); its knobs and XY axes can be mapped through [MIDI Learn](../midi-learn/) like hardware.
The XY pad and tilt are mapped per track, so each track's XY plays its own sound.

Several phones can play at once, each on its own track — the track list and the mixer show who plays
what.

## What phones may do

In [Settings ▸ Remote](../../preferences/remote/): **Play notes only** (pads and keys; mixer, transport
and mappings are read-only) or **Play and control the project** (also mixer, transport, macros, XY and
Session).

<Aside type="caution">
If phones can't connect: check they're on the same network (guest Wi-Fi often keeps devices apart —
use the phone's hotspot or a USB cable instead), that Remote is on, and that your firewall allows Nota
on the port shown in Settings.
</Aside>
