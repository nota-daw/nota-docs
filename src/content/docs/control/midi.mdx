Plug in a MIDI keyboard or controller and play: Nota listens to every connected MIDI input by
default. Notes go to the selected instrument track; arm the track and press Record to
[record](../../editing/recording/) them.

## Choosing inputs

**Settings ▸ MIDI** lists the connected MIDI inputs. Switch one off to stop Nota listening to it; new
devices are on by default. Plug a controller in while Settings is open and reopen it to see it.

<Shot id="preferences/midi" alt="Settings ▸ MIDI with the list of MIDI inputs" />

The **MIDI** button in the transport bar has a dot that flashes when a controller sends something —
handy for checking a controller that's connected but silent.

## Pitch wheel, aftertouch and MPE

A keyboard's **pitch wheel**, **aftertouch** and **CC74** reach Nota's synths and plug-ins: the wheel
bends the whole instrument by the synth's own bend range (2 semitones, or the patch's **Bend Range**
where it has one).

An **MPE controller** (Seaboard, LinnStrument, Osmose, Sensel and the like) gives every note its own
channel, so each finger bends, presses and slides its note on its own. Nota understands it out of the
box: channels 2–16 carry one note each, channel 1 stays an ordinary keyboard. These synths play per-note
expression:

| Synth | Bend | Pressure | Slide (CC74) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Nota Synth](../../devices/instruments/synth/), [Volt](../../devices/instruments/volt/) | The note's pitch | Louder, filter opens | Filter cutoff |
| [Aurora](../../devices/instruments/aurora/) | The note's pitch | Louder, filter opens | Wavetable position |
| [Operator](../../devices/instruments/operator/) | The note's pitch | Louder, more FM | FM depth |
| [Pentad](../../devices/instruments/pentad/) | The note's pitch | Poly aftertouch: louder, filter opens by **Aftertouch Cutoff** | Filter cutoff |
| [Physical](../../devices/instruments/physical/) | Retunes the ringing body | Bows it — a held note sustains | How long it rings |
| [Keys](../../devices/instruments/keys/) | Retunes the tine | Drives the tine harder into the pickup — more bark | Shifts the pickup's symmetry |

An Instrument Rack passes expression to its chains. A plug-in gets the wheel, channel pressure,
polyphonic aftertouch and the sustain pedal, but not per-note bend. Switch MPE off, or change the per-note bend range, in
[Settings ▸ MIDI](../../preferences/midi/); a controller that announces its own range overrides it.
When you [record](../../editing/recording/), each note keeps its bend, pressure and slide: the take
plays back as you played it, the expression travels with the note when you move, copy or quantize it,
and the [piano roll](../../editing/piano-roll/#recorded-mpe) draws it. The pitch wheel, channel pressure
and sustain pedal aren't recorded.

A keyboard's **sustain pedal** (CC64) holds the notes of [Nota Keys](../../devices/instruments/keys/)
and reaches plug-ins; it is played live and isn't recorded into clips — automate
Keys' **Pedal** instead.

## No MIDI keyboard? Use the computer keyboard

| Keys | Play |
|---|---|
| <Kbd>A</Kbd> <Kbd>S</Kbd> <Kbd>D</Kbd> <Kbd>F</Kbd> <Kbd>G</Kbd> <Kbd>H</Kbd> <Kbd>J</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd> | White keys, from C4 |
| <Kbd>W</Kbd> <Kbd>E</Kbd> · <Kbd>T</Kbd> <Kbd>Y</Kbd> <Kbd>U</Kbd> | Black keys |
| <Kbd>Z</Kbd> / <Kbd>X</Kbd> | Octave down / up |
| <Kbd>C</Kbd> / <Kbd>V</Kbd> | Velocity down / up |

It works even while a plug-in's window has focus. The pressed key lights up on the piano roll.

## Knobs and faders

To make a controller's knobs, faders and pads move Nota's controls, use [MIDI Learn](../midi-learn/).
A gamepad and your phone can play and control Nota too — see [Gamepads](../gamepads/) and
[Nota Remote](../nota-remote/).
