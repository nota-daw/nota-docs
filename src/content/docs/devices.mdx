Every track has a **device chain**: the devices that make and shape its sound, left to right. Select
a track to see its chain in the panel at the bottom.

<Shot id="views/detail-panel" alt="The device chain of the Keys track: Nota Pentad, then Nota EQ-8" />

**MIDI effects → instrument → audio effects.** MIDI effects (an arpeggiator, a chord generator)
change the notes before the instrument plays them; the instrument turns notes into sound; audio
effects process that sound. An audio track has no instrument — only audio effects.

## Adding devices

- **Double-click** an instrument or effect in the browser — an effect goes onto the selected track.
- **Drag** it from the browser onto a track, or into the chain where you want it.
- Dropping an instrument onto an instrument track **replaces** its instrument.

## The card

Each device is a card with a header — name, preset picker (**‹ Init ⌄ ›**), the **S / L** size switch
where the device has one, and the on / off (bypass) switch — over its controls. Knobs, sliders and
graphs work the same everywhere: drag, <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> for fine steps, double-click to reset; graphs
are controls too (drag the nodes, curves and handles).

| Do this | To |
|---|---|
| Drag the header | Move the device in the chain |
| Click a device · <Kbd>Delete</Kbd> | Remove it |
| Right-click the header | **Move left** / **Move right**, **Copy**, **Paste**, **Delete**, presets, **Save preset**, A / B compare, and **Documentation** — this device's page in the manual |
| Click the power switch | Bypass the device |

**A / B compare** keeps two versions of a device's settings: switch between **A** and **B** to compare,
**Copy A to B** to start a variation.

## Presets

Every built-in device ships with factory presets — the arrows step through them, the name opens the
list. In the browser, a device expands into folders of its factory presets (Pads, Bass, Leads…).
**Save preset** stores your own; they appear in the browser's **Presets** tab. A preview in the browser plays an effect preset through a short demo, so you can
audition before you load it.

## Sidechain

Compressor, Dynamic EQ-8, Ceiling, Level, Shutter, Auto Filter and Prism can listen to **another
track** — pick it as the device's sidechain source (pre or post that track's fader). Nota Flux reacts
to another track, and Auto Shift can follow another track's notes, the same way.

## Plug-ins

VST3 (and AU on macOS) plug-ins sit in the chain like built-in devices. Their card shows the
parameters; **Open editor** opens the plug-in's own window. See [Plug-ins](../plugins/).

<Aside>
Any control on any device can be [automated](../editing/automation/), mapped to a controller with
[MIDI Learn](../control/midi-learn/), or patched with CV in the [Modular view](../views/modular/).
</Aside>
