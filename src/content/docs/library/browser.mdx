The browser on the left of the main window holds everything you can bring into a project. Its
tabs are the icons down its left edge; the button at the bottom collapses it (also **View ▸ Toggle
browser**).

<Shot id="start-here/browser-instruments" alt="The browser, Instruments tab" width={300} />

## Tabs

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Instruments** | Nota's instruments (**BUILT-IN**) and your plug-in instruments (**PLUG-INS**). Each device opens into folders of its factory presets — Pads, Bass, Leads, Keys & Plucks… — with **Init** on top |
| **Audio effects** | Built-in and plug-in effects, with their presets — Vocals, Drums, Mix & Bus, Mastering… |
| **MIDI effects** | Arpeggiator, chord, scale, length, velocity, random, with their presets |
| **Files** | Your sample library as a folder tree — see [Smart samples](../smart-samples/) |
| **Presets** | The presets you saved yourself (right-click a device header ▸ **Save preset**) |
| **Projects** | The projects in your projects folder — open, reveal or delete |
| **MIDI map** | Every control mapped to a controller, gamepad or phone — see [MIDI Learn](../../control/midi-learn/) |
| **History** | The open project's [versions](../../basics/version-history/) |

## Searching

The search box filters the tab and counts the matches; search opens the preset folders around the
hits, and a folder name ("Mastering") lists that folder across every device. The status line at the
bottom counts what's shown — `16 built-in · 0 plug-ins`.

The ⋮ button beside the search box holds view options — type tags, grouping by source, favourites
first — and the tag editor.

## Favourites and tags

Star (★) a device or preset to make it a favourite — favourites sort to the top of their section.
**Tags** let you group things your own way: assign them from the right-click menu, edit their title and
colour in the tag editor, and filter with the chips under the search box.

## Getting things into the project

| Do this | To |
|---|---|
| Double-click an instrument | A new track with it (and an empty one-bar clip) |
| Double-click an effect | Add it to the selected track |
| Double-click a MIDI file | Put it on a new track |
| Drag onto a track or empty space | Add the instrument, effect, sample or loop there |
| Drag onto a device chain | Insert the effect between cards (a bar marks the spot) or replace the card under the pointer |
| Drag onto a Session slot | Put a sample or MIDI file in that slot |
| Drag onto a rack chain or a pad | Load it there |

Effects and presets can also be dropped onto a return track or the master.

## Hearing before loading

The **preview player** at the bottom of the browser plays samples, presets, kits and devices before
you load them — see [Preview player](../preview-player/).
