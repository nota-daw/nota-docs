The player at the bottom of the browser lets you hear something before it goes into the project.
It appears once you use a list.

## Samples (Files tab)

The file's real waveform — click it to play from that point, hover for the time — with **Play** /
**Stop**, the time, **Loop**, **Auto** (play each file as you select it) and a preview volume
(double-click it to reset to 0 dB). <Kbd>↑</Kbd> <Kbd>↓</Kbd> step through the files; with Auto on,
each one plays as you land on it.

## Presets, kits and devices

Under the **Instruments**, **Audio effects**, **MIDI effects** and **Presets** tabs the same player
plays the selected factory preset, saved preset, drum kit or device. These tabs have their own
**Auto**, off by default — press Play, or turn Auto on to hear each one as you select it. Sounds are
rendered in a few milliseconds and the next row is prepared ahead, so stepping through a folder is
instant.

- **Instruments** play a phrase that suits them — a chord for pads, a riff for basses, a line for
  leads, an arpeggio for plucks and bells.
- **Drum kits** (Drum Rack and Nota Rhythm) play a groove on their own pads.
- **MIDI effects** play a phrase that shows what they do.
- **Audio effects** process a **demo track** — Drums, Keys or Bass, or genre loops (Pop, House,
  Techno, Hip-Hop, Trap, Drum & Bass, Synthwave, Ambient). A track that suits the effect is picked for
  you; the track button picks another (scroll over it to step through them), or the sample you last
  selected in Files.

**WAVE / SPEC** switches the window between the waveform and a live spectrum.

Instrument Racks and plug-in presets aren't previewed — the player says why.
