**Settings ▸ Downloads ▸ Sample Packs** installs free sample packs: drum kits, a TR-808, sampled
basses, guitars, pianos, horns and more. Every pack is licensed for any music, commercial releases
included.

<Shot id="preferences/downloads-samples" alt="Settings ▸ Downloads ▸ Sample Packs" />

## Installing

Filter by **All**, **One-shots**, **Instruments** or **Installed** and search by name. Each row says
what the pack is (one-shots, loops, instrument, mixed), who made it (**Credit**) and its size.

- **Install** — downloads the pack from its author's own page, checks it against the registry's
  checksum and unpacks only the samples and their docs. Nota checks there is room first.
- **Update to …** — when a newer version is out.
- **Show** — opens the pack's folder; **Source** — the pack's page.
- **Remove** — deletes the pack's folder.

Packs go into a **Downloaded** folder inside your Samples folder, so they show up in the browser's
**Files** tab — and are analysed first by [Smart samples](../smart-samples/).

## Nota Kits

The one-shots of the 25 factory drum kits are synthesised on first launch (not shipped as audio) and
appear in the Files tab under **Nota Kits**, ready to drag anywhere a sample goes.
