One page for three kinds of download, picked with the switch beside its title:

| Side | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Plug-ins** | Open-source VST3 plug-ins from the Nota plug-in registry — see [Downloading plug-ins](../../plugins/get-plugins/) |
| **Sample Packs** | Free sample packs, licensed for any music — see [Sample packs](../../library/sample-packs/) |
| **AI Models** | htdemucs and basic-pitch — see [AI models](../../ai/models/) |

<Shot id="preferences/downloads" alt="Settings ▸ Downloads ▸ Plug-ins" />

Everything downloads from its authors' own release, is checked against a pinned checksum, and only
then unpacked — installers are never run. Filters and search stay at the top while the list scrolls.
A download keeps going after you close Settings: its progress moves to the main window's status line,
with a × to cancel.
