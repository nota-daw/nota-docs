| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | **Ember Graphite** (dark, the default), **Ember Paper** (light) or **System** (follows your computer's appearance and switches with it) |
| **Waveform** | How audio clips draw in the Arrangement: **Bars** spaces the peaks out so the rhythm reads; **Solid** draws every pixel |
| **AI control (MCP)** | Let an AI assistant work in Nota — the switch, the port, the status and **Copy config**. See [AI control over MCP](../../ai/mcp/) |
