- **Plugin scan folders** — extra folders to search for VST3 plug-ins, besides the system's standard
  ones and the folder Nota installs registry plug-ins into. **Add folder…** and **Remove**. AU plug-ins
  come from the system registry.
- **Rescan plugins** — loads every plug-in that isn't in the catalogue yet, counting them as it goes
  (**Scanning plugins · 312 of 1,000 · …**), then reports how many plug-ins Nota knows and how many
  couldn't be loaded. It also retries the plug-ins that failed before, which ordinary scans skip.

See [Scanning plug-ins](../../plugins/scanning/).
