The Shortcuts page lists every key and gesture in groups — File, Transport, Arrangement & editing,
Tracks, Session, Piano roll, Play notes, Browser · preview, Gamepad and Mouse. Type in **Filter
shortcuts** to find one.

The same list, in full, is in [Keyboard shortcuts](../../reference/shortcuts/).

## Velocity wheel

At the top of the page, **Velocity wheel** sets how <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> + wheel changes the velocity of
a note in the [piano roll](../../editing/piano-roll/#velocity):

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Direction** | **Wheel up lowers the velocity** — off by default, so wheel up raises it |
| **Step** | How far one notch moves the velocity: **Off**, or 0.25× to 2× in steps of 0.25×. **1×** (the default) is one MIDI step per notch |

With **Off**, <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> + wheel only scrolls, over notes too. The setting applies to every
piano roll and is remembered.
