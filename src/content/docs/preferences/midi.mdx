**MIDI inputs** lists every connected controller with a switch. Switch one off to stop Nota listening
to it; new devices are on by default. If nothing is listed, connect a controller and reopen Settings.

**MPE** — **Expression** switches per-note expression from MPE controllers (on by default: a keyboard
on channel 1 plays as without it). **Bend range** is how far a note bends at full travel on channels
2–16: ±12, ±24, ±48 (the default) or ±96 semitones. A controller that announces its own range
overrides it. See [Pitch wheel, aftertouch and MPE](../../control/midi/#pitch-wheel-aftertouch-and-mpe).

See [MIDI controllers](../../control/midi/) and [MIDI Learn](../../control/midi-learn/).
