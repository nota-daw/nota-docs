The Assistant answers questions about Nota from this manual, right in the app. It sits on the
right of the main window, the full height of the work area, and works offline — the manual
ships inside Nota, so no internet and no AI model are needed.


<Shot id="basics/assistant" alt="The Assistant panel on the right of the main window, answering “how do I record several takes” with sections of the manual" />

## Open the Assistant

<At id="ui:transport.assistant" /> Click the speech-bubble key on the transport bar, choose
**View ▸ Assistant**, or press <Kbd>⌘ ⇧ H</Kbd> (<Kbd>Ctrl Shift H</Kbd> on Windows and Linux).
The same again closes it. Drag its left edge to make it wider or narrower; Nota remembers the
width and whether it was open. On a narrow window the key moves into the transport bar's
[⋯ menu](../transport/#when-the-bar-is-too-narrow).

## Ask a question

Type what you want to do in your own words — *how do I duck the bass when the kick hits*,
*как записать несколько дублей* — and press <Kbd>Return</Kbd> (<Kbd>⇧ Return</Kbd> starts a new
line). The reply quotes the two or three sections of the manual that answer it, each with a
link to its page. A question in Russian gets the Russian manual, one in English the English
manual. Typos, the wrong keyboard layout and other word forms are forgiven. If nothing in the
manual fits, the reply says so and links to the manual.

Replies read like the manual: headings, lists, tables, key caps and the note, tip and caution
boxes. Select text to copy part of it, or use the copy key under a reply for all of it.
<At id="ui:assistant.clear" /> The bin key at the top clears the conversation; it is never saved
with the project and is gone when you quit.

## Show me

Under a section that explains how to do something, **Show me** walks you through it. Each step
opens what is needed — the right view, the Mixer, the Detail panel or a browser tab — and
outlines the control in orange; the panel says *Step 2 of 4* with **Back**, **Next** and
**Done**. Do the step on the control itself, then press **Next**. If a step needs something
first, such as a selected track, the panel says so and points at where to do it. Show me only
shows: it never changes your project and never starts or stops playback. <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> stops
it.


<Shot id="basics/assistant-show-me" alt="Show me walking through a step: the browser's Audio effects tab is outlined, the panel shows Step 1 of 1" />

## Search by meaning

Words alone find most answers. For questions phrased very differently from the manual, install
**Manual search** in [Settings ▸ Downloads ▸ AI Models](../../preferences/downloads/) — the
multilingual-e5-small model, about 135 MB, which also runs offline. The first time the panel
opens after that, Nota reads the manual once in the background (under a minute); until then,
and while you record, the Assistant answers by words.
