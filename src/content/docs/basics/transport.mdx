The transport bar runs along the top of the main window. It holds what plays the song and
the settings that apply to the whole project.

<Shot id="basics/transport-bar" alt="The transport bar" />

## Play, stop, record

| Control | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Play** | <Kbd>Space</Kbd> | Start playback; <Kbd>Space</Kbd> again stops. |
| **Stop** | <Kbd>Return</Kbd> | Stop. Press it again to jump back to the start of the song. |
| **Record** | <Kbd>⌘ R</Kbd> | Record onto the armed tracks — see [Recording](../../editing/recording/). |

When you stop, the playhead returns to where playback started, so you can listen to the same
passage again and again. Click in the ruler to move the playhead somewhere else.

The **position** readout shows bars.beats.sixteenths. Click it to switch to minutes:seconds
and back.

## Tempo, time signature and key

- **BPM** — drag up or down to change the tempo in whole steps, or double-click it and type a
  value with up to two decimals. The range is 20–300 BPM.
- **SIG** — the time signature. Drag each number; the lower one moves in powers of two
  (2, 4, 8, 16).
- **KEY** — the project key, saved with the project. Click it to pick a key, or **No key**.
  With **Transpose to project key** on in the Files tab's menu, loops you drop on the
  Arrangement are transposed to it; loops are always warped to the project tempo. The phone
  keyboard of [Nota Remote](../../control/nota-remote/) uses it too.

## Loop

**LOOP** shows the loop range set on the ruler. <Kbd>⌘ L</Kbd> turns looping on or off — and
if clips or a time range are selected, it first sets the loop to them. Drag along the ruler to
draw a loop range by hand.

## Metronome and count-in

The metronome button (<Kbd>⌘ M</Kbd>) clicks on every beat while you play or record.

**Right-click** it to choose a **count-in**: off, 1, 2 or 4 bars of clicks before Play and
Record from stop. The count-in sounds even with the metronome off, the position readout counts
the bars before the start (−2.1, −1.4 …), and the clicks are never recorded. Stop cancels a
count-in. Nota remembers the setting.

## Grid, snap and follow

- **GRID** — click to cycle the snap grid for moving clips: 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1 bar.
- **Snap** (the magnet) — clips snap to the grid while you drag them. Hold <Kbd>⌥</Kbd> while
  dragging to place a clip freely just this once.
- **Follow** — the Arrangement scrolls to keep the playhead in view. Also in **View ▸ Follow
  playhead**.
- **Automation** — shows the automation lanes of the tracks (<Kbd>⌘ ⇧ A</Kbd>). The button
  next to it hands controls you've moved by hand back to their automation — see
  [Automation](../../editing/automation/).

## The right side

- **MIDI** — [MIDI Learn](../../control/midi-learn/): click it, click a control in Nota, then
  move a knob on your controller.
- **Remote** — shows the QR code that connects a phone ([Nota Remote](../../control/nota-remote/)).
- **MST** — master volume, with the master meter underneath.

<Aside type="tip">
The transport keys work in every Nota window, including a detached device window and the
Mixer.
</Aside>

## Launch quantize

In the [Session](../../views/session/) view, clips and scenes start on the next bar (or
another division) so they always land in time. That setting, **Quant**, lives in the Session
toolbar.
