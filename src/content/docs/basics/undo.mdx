## Undo and redo

**Edit ▸ Undo** (<Kbd>⌘ Z</Kbd>) and **Edit ▸ Redo** (<Kbd>⌘ ⇧ Z</Kbd>) cover every edit:
notes and clips, tracks, devices and their settings, automation, Session clips and scenes,
arrangement sections. A gesture is one step — dragging several clips, or turning a knob from
one value to another, undoes in one go. So is switching a device between **A** and **B**,
typing a value, one movement of a MIDI controller knob (it ends when the knob rests for a
second), one touch on Nota Remote, and each change an AI assistant makes.

Mute, solo, sends and the devices inside a rack are not in the history.

To go back further than undo reaches — to yesterday's mix, say — use
[version history](../version-history/).

## Setting values

Knobs, sliders and numbers across Nota work the same way:

| Do this | To |
|---|---|
| Drag up / down | Change the value |
| Drag with <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> or <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> held | Change it in fine steps |
| Double-click a knob | Reset it to its default |
| Double-click a slider's value | Type an exact value |

When you type, units work: `-6`, `1.5k`, `250 ms`, `-3 dB`, `L 30`, `1/16`. <Kbd>Return</Kbd>
sets the value, <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> cancels. This works in device cards, the clip editor and rack
macros.

Track faders and pan bars reset to their default on a double-click too.

## Automation and MIDI

Any value you can turn can also be [automated](../../editing/automation/) or mapped to a
hardware control with [MIDI Learn](../../control/midi-learn/).
