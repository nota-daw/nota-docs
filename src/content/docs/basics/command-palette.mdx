The command palette finds anything in Nota by typing: a menu command, a track, a built-in
device, a plug-in, a preset or a kit. Press <Kbd>⌘ ⇧ P</Kbd> (<Kbd>Ctrl Shift P</Kbd> on
Windows and Linux) in any window — even while a text field has the focus — and press it again
to close.

<Shot id="basics/command-palette" alt="The command palette over the main window: the query «тёплый пэд» lists warm pad presets, each with the reason it was found" />

## Put a device where you are

What you pick goes to **the place you opened the palette from**, and the pill at the right of
the field says where before you press <Kbd>Return</Kbd> — for example
**→ Bass · after Nota Compressor** or **→ new MIDI track**.

| Opened from | Where a device or preset goes |
|---|---|
| **Devices** panel (docked or in its own window) | Right after the selected card; with no card selected, the end of its section (MIDI FX or audio effects) |
| **Modular** | The track shown in Modular; a Modular node (LFO, ADSR, Math…) lands on the canvas unconnected, under the pointer or in the middle of the view |
| **Arrangement**, **Session**, **Mixer**, **Browser** | The selected track, at the end of its chain |
| **Piano roll** | The track of the clip being edited |

An instrument on a track that already has one **replaces** it — the clips, the other devices
and the volume stay. On an **Instrument Rack** it adds a chain, on a **Drum Rack** it fills
the next free pad. With no track selected, an instrument or instrument preset makes a new
track; an effect shows **Select a track first** and can't be applied. A MIDI effect can't go
on an audio track, and the row says so.

<Shot id="basics/command-palette-replace" alt="The palette with ⌥ held: the pill reads «→ Bass · replaces Nota Compressor»" />

Hold a modifier to change what <Kbd>Return</Kbd> does — the pill and the footer follow:

- <Kbd>⌘ Return</Kbd> — an instrument or instrument preset on a **new track**; on a track, open it in **Devices**.
- <Kbd>⌥ Return</Kbd> — **replace** the selected device instead of inserting after it.
- <Kbd>⇧ Return</Kbd> — apply and **keep the palette open**; the next device goes after the one you just added.

Every apply is one undo step. A short note at the bottom of the window confirms what
happened and has an **Undo** button (or press <Kbd>⌘ Z</Kbd>).

## Search by name or by meaning

- **Names, loosely.** Letters in order are enough: **afl** finds **Nota Auto Filter**, **nv**
  finds **Nota Vintage**. The matched letters light up. "Nota" at the front of a name doesn't
  count, so type the distinctive word.
- **One typo per word** is forgiven (**reverv**, **compresor**).
- **The wrong keyboard layout** is read too: **кумуки** typed with the Russian layout on finds
  **reverb**, and a line under the filters says so.
- **Meaning, in English or Russian.** Every device, plug-in and preset knows what it is, how it
  sounds, what part it plays and what it's used on. So **warm pad**, **тёплый пэд**,
  **reverb for vocals**, **компрессор на барабаны**, **sidechain** or **bass for techno** all
  find something — and a result found by meaning rather than by name shows why, for example
  **warm · pad**. A name that matches exactly always comes first.

## Filter by type

Type a prefix, or press <Kbd>Tab</Kbd> / <Kbd>⇧ Tab</Kbd> to step through the chips above the list:

| Prefix | Finds |
|---|---|
| `>` | Actions — menu and view commands, transport, Settings pages, each with its shortcut |
| `@` | Tracks — including groups, returns and the master |
| `+` | Devices and plug-ins (a plug-in shows its vendor and format) |
| `#` | Presets — factory, your own, and drum kits |
| `~` | Modular nodes (only when opened from Modular) |

An action that can't run right now (**Quantize Notes** with no MIDI clip open) stays hidden
unless you type its name; then it shows greyed out with the reason.

With nothing typed, the palette shows **Recent** — the last five things you applied from it —
and suggestions for where you are: effects in Devices, nodes in Modular, **Launch Scene** and
**Stop All Clips** in Session. Things you use often, from the palette or the browser, rank
higher over time.

## Keys

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <Kbd>↑ ↓</Kbd> <Kbd>PgUp PgDn</Kbd> <Kbd>Home End</Kbd> | Move through the results |
| <Kbd>Return</Kbd> | Apply (see the modifiers above) |
| <Kbd>Tab</Kbd> | Next type filter |
| <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> | Close and go back where you were |

The mouse works too: hovering selects a row, a click applies it, a click outside closes the
palette. <Kbd>Space</Kbd> types a space in the field — it doesn't start playback while the
palette is open.

<Aside>
The palette, the menu and the keyboard shortcuts run the same commands, so a shortcut shown
in the palette is the one in the menu and in **Settings ▸ Shortcuts**.
</Aside>
