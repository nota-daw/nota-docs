Separate Stems pulls a mix apart into **Drums**, **Bass**, **Other** and **Vocals**. Remix a song,
practise over it without the bass, sample a drum break, or turn a vocal into MIDI.

<Steps>

1. Right-click an audio clip in the Arrangement ▸ **Separate Stems…**. The first time, Nota offers to
   download the **htdemucs** [model](../models/).

2. Wait for the progress window — a three-minute song takes well under a minute. **Cancel** stops it.

3. A group of four tracks appears right under the clip's track — Drums, Bass, Other, Vocals — each
   clip lined up with the original, warp, reverse, fades and ADSR included.

</Steps>

The original clip is switched off, not deleted, and the whole operation undoes in one step.

<Aside type="tip">
Then [convert a stem to MIDI](../../editing/midi-tools/) — a clean vocal or bass stem converts far
better than the full mix.
</Aside>
