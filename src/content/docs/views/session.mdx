The Session view is a grid of clips: a **column** per track, a **row** per scene. Each clip
loops until you launch another one in its column, so you can try parts against each other,
jam, and build a song live.

## Launching

- **Click** a slot to select it; click its **▶** triangle to launch it.
- Launch a whole row with the **▶** next to its scene name on the left.
- The **■** button at the bottom of a column stops that track; **Stop All** (or **Stop All
  Clips** under the grid) stops everything.
- <Kbd>Return</Kbd> launches whatever is selected.

Clips and scenes start in time with the music: on the next bar, or whatever **QUANT** in the
toolbar says — **None**, 1/16 … 1/2, **1 Bar**, 2, 4 or 8 bars. A clip's progress pie shows
where it is in its own loop.

## The grid

Columns carry the track's name and colour; a clip has a name, a colour, a preview of its notes
or waveform, and its length in bars. A group track is one column you can launch, and it folds
its tracks away. Returns and the master close the row.

| Do this | To |
|---|---|
| Arrow keys | Move the selection between slots and scenes |
| Double-click a slot | Edit the clip; an empty MIDI slot gets a new clip |
| Drag a clip to another slot | Move it — with <Kbd>⌥</Kbd>, copy it |
| Drag a scene up or down | Reorder scenes |
| <Kbd>⌘ C</Kbd> <Kbd>⌘ X</Kbd> <Kbd>⌘ V</Kbd> | Copy, cut, paste a clip (a cut clip moves when pasted) |
| <Kbd>⌘ D</Kbd> | Duplicate a clip into the slot below, or a scene below itself |
| <Kbd>⌘ I</Kbd> | Insert a scene below the selection |
| <Kbd>F2</Kbd> | Rename the selected clip or scene |
| <Kbd>Delete</Kbd> | Delete the selected clip or scene |
| Double-click a column header | Rename the track |
| Right-click a header, a slot or the empty grid | Add an instrument, audio or return track, rename or delete one |

You can insert, duplicate, capture and move scenes while the music plays. **Capture Scene**
copies the clips playing right now into a new scene.

Audio slots are full audio clips: double-click one for the same
[audio clip editor](../../editing/audio-clips/) as in the Arrangement.

## The inspector

The panel on the right edits whatever is selected.

**A clip:**

- **Launch** mode — **Trigger** (start and keep playing), **Gate** (play while held),
  **Toggle** (start / stop on each launch) or **Repeat** (retrigger in time while held).
- **Quantize** — its own launch quantize, or the global one.
- **Legato** — take over from the clip that was playing at the same position instead of
  starting from the top.
- **Loop** — off makes it a one-shot.
- **Velocity %** — how much the launch velocity (from a pad or key) scales the clip.
- **Follow action** — what happens after the clip has played for a while: two actions **A** and
  **B** with a chance between them and a time. Actions: **Next**, **Again**, **Previous**,
  **First**, **Last**, **Any**, **Other**, **Stop** and **Jump to** a scene.
- **Color**, **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Delete**, **To Arrangement**.

**A scene:** a name and colour, a **Tempo** and **Signature** that apply when it launches
(**Launch without a tempo change** skips them), and **Next after** — move on to the next scene
after a number of bars.

**An empty slot:** **Insert MIDI clip**, **Paste**, **Record here**, and **Stop button** — turn
it off so launching this scene keeps that track playing instead of stopping it.

The **Follow** switch in the toolbar turns all follow actions on or off at once.

## Recording into a slot

Arm a track, select a scene, and click **Session Rec**: Nota records into that track's slot in
the selected scene. Click **Session Rec** again to keep the take — open-ended takes are cut to
whole bars. Or set **FIXED** to 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars, and the take stops by itself.

## The mixer under the grid

Under the grid sits a mixer for every column. Show or hide its sections with **I/O**,
**Sends** and **Mixer** in the toolbar: inputs and outputs, the send to each return, pan,
volume, mute, solo and arm.

## Session and Arrangement

A track plays either its Session clip or its Arrangement clips. Launching a slot takes the
track over; **Back to Arrangement** hands every track back, and the **ARR** badge on a column
hands back just that track.

Clips copy both ways — <Kbd>⌘ ⇧ C</Kbd> sends the selected clip, group slot or scene to the
Arrangement at the playhead. See [Copy, paste and drag](../../editing/clipboard/).
