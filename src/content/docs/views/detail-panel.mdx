The panel under the Arrangement or Session shows what you're working on in detail. Select a
track and it shows the track's devices; double-click a clip and it opens the clip.

<Shot id="views/detail-panel" alt="The detail panel showing the Keys track: Nota Pentad and Nota EQ-8" />

## Devices, Pattern, Clip

The tabs in its top-left corner switch what it shows — <Kbd>Tab</Kbd> cycles them:

- **Devices** — the track's [device chain](../../devices/): MIDI effects, the instrument and
  the effects, left to right. Scroll sideways when they don't fit.
- **Pattern** — the step grid of a MIDI clip on a Drum Rack track. The tab is there only on a
  Drum Rack track.
- **Clip** — the selected clip in the [MIDI](../../editing/piano-roll/) or
  [audio](../../editing/audio-clips/) clip editor.

Drag the panel's top edge to make **Pattern** and **Clip** taller or shorter; each keeps the
height you gave it. Double-click the edge to put the tab back at its default height — the
browser's edge and a docked Mixer's top edge reset the same way. **Devices** has a fixed
height that fits the cards, so it can't be resized.

## The clip editor filling the window

On **Clip** and **Pattern**, the expand button at the right of the header — or
<Kbd>⌘ ⇧ F</Kbd>, or **View ▸ Clip editor fills the window** — gives the editor the whole
window: the browser, the Arrangement (or Session, Modular) and a docked Mixer fold away. The
same button, now pointing inwards, or the same key brings them back at the heights they had.
Picking a view, showing the Mixer or the browser, or switching to **Devices** also goes back
to the split.

The header names the track and its type; for an instrument track it also shows the delay the
chain adds (**PDC**), which Nota compensates automatically.

On the right: **Freeze** and **Live freeze** ([freezing a track](../../basics/tracks/#freezing-a-track)),
the ⧉ button and × to close the panel. **View ▸ Toggle detail panel** shows or hides it.

## The panel in its own window

⧉ moves the panel into a window of its own — useful on a second screen. That window shows the
**clip editor on top and the device chain underneath**, both at once, with a splitter between
them. Close it and the panel docks back under the view.

## Device windows

A device inside a rack — a Drum Rack pad's sampler, an Instrument Rack chain's synth, an
effect in an Audio Effect Rack chain or on a Nota Rhythm voice — opens in a window of its own.
Its title bar carries the device and track name and the preset picker, plus the **S / L** size
switch and the bypass switch where the device has them; the full device sits underneath, with a
status line at the bottom.
