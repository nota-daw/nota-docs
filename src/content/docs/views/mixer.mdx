**View ▸ Mixer** or <Kbd>⌘ ⇧ M</Kbd> shows the Mixer, and the same again hides it. It docks as
a panel at the bottom of the main window, under the Arrangement, Session or Modular view, so
you can mix and still see the song.

<Shot id="views/mixer-docked" alt="The Mixer docked under the Arrangement: a strip per track, the returns and the master" />

## Docked or in a window

- **Resize** the docked Mixer by dragging its top edge. It stays where it is when you switch
  between Arrangement, Session and Modular.
- The **Devices** / **Clip** panel can be open at the same time: the Mixer sits above it, and
  each has its own edge to drag. In a short window the Mixer gets shorter first.
- **To a second screen**: the ⧉ button at the top right of the docked Mixer moves it into a
  window of its own. The window's dock button (top right of its title bar) brings it back.
  **View ▸ Mixer in its own window** switches between the two as well.
- **×** on the panel, or closing the window (<Kbd>Esc</Kbd> works there), hides the Mixer.

Nota remembers whether the Mixer was shown, docked or in a window, and how tall the panel
was, and opens it the same way next time.

<Shot id="views/mixer" alt="The Mixer in its own window: a strip per track, the return and the master" />

## A channel strip

Top to bottom, each track gets:

- its **name** and colour;
- **I/O** — where the track listens: the audio input (**In 1** …) and **Monitor** (**Auto** or
  **In**) for audio tracks, **MIDI In** / **MIDI To** for instrument tracks; group and return
  strips show what feeds them;
- **Sends** — one knob per return track;
- **Pan** and the **volume** fader with its meter. Drag the gain readout or double-click it to
  type a value; double-click the fader to reset it;
- **Mute**, **Solo** and **arm** buttons.

Return tracks come after the tracks, and the **Master** strip closes the row. Its fader is the
same master volume as the **Master** control in the transport bar and the Master row of the
Arrangement — move one and the others follow; it is saved with the project. **Show** at the
bottom switches the **Sends** and **I/O** sections on or off.

Faders, pan and the mute, solo and arm buttons follow changes made anywhere else — a track
header, the Session mixer, automation, undo or a MIDI controller.

## When there is little room

The strips fit the height they get. As the Mixer gets shorter, the **I/O** rows go first, then
the **Sends**, then the fader gets shorter; the name, meter, fader, pan and buttons always
stay. A section hidden for lack of room shows its **Show** button outlined instead of filled —
click it to show the section anyway (the strips then scroll up and down), click again to turn
it off.

## Sends and returns

A return track is a shared effect bus. Add one (right-click the empty space below the tracks ▸
**Add return track**), put a Reverb or Delay on it, then turn up each track's send to it.
Every track goes to the same reverb, and one fader on the return sets how much of it you hear.
