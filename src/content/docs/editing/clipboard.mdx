<Kbd>⌘ C</Kbd>, <Kbd>⌘ X</Kbd>, <Kbd>⌘ V</Kbd> and <Kbd>⌘ D</Kbd> (duplicate) work on whatever
you clicked last:

| Where | What they copy |
|---|---|
| Arrangement lanes | Clips, or an automation range in automation mode |
| Track headers | Whole tracks — a group with its tracks; pasted tracks land after the selected one |
| Session grid | A clip or a scene |
| Clip editor | Notes |
| Device chain | Devices (right-click a device header ▸ **Copy**) |

Every paste is one undo step.

## Between the Arrangement and Session

The clipboard carries clips both ways: copy clips on the timeline and paste them on a Session
slot, or copy a slot and paste it on the timeline.

**⌘ ⇧ C** sends the selection straight across — also **Copy to Session** / **Copy to
Arrangement** in the right-click menus and the Edit menu:

- **Arrangement → Session:** the clips land in the first scene row where they all fit — each
  track's clips down its column in time order — adding scenes if needed. **Copy to Scene** picks
  the scene yourself.
- **Session → Arrangement:** a clip, a group slot or a whole scene lands on the timeline at the
  playhead, without covering clips that are already there.

Notes, clip envelopes, names, warp, pitch and gain come along.

## Audio clips into instruments

Drag an audio clip from the Arrangement onto the row of a track with a **Sampler**, **Nota
Grain**, **Drum Rack** or **Instrument Rack** and its audio loads there — a Drum Rack takes it
on the next free pad. Rest over an instrument track while dragging and its devices open, so a
particular pad is one more move away.

Pull a clip out of the Arrangement and it becomes a **sample** you can drop anywhere a sample
goes: a Drum Rack pad, a Grain, a Nota Rhythm voice, a Session slot. The clip itself stays where
it was; the sample is the part of the clip you hear.

## Dragging from the browser

Anything in the browser can be dragged into the project: instruments and effects onto tracks or
device chains, samples and loops onto tracks or slots, MIDI files onto instrument tracks,
presets onto devices. See [The browser](../../library/browser/).

<Aside type="tip">
Hold <Kbd>⌥</Kbd> while dragging a clip in the Session grid to copy it instead of moving it.
</Aside>
