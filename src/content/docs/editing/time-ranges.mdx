A **time range** is a stretch of the song across one or more tracks — not whole clips, but
"bars 9 to 17". It's how you rework the structure of a song.

## Selecting a range

- <Kbd>⇧</Kbd>-drag in the Arrangement lanes — the drag can start on empty space or on a clip.
  The edges snap to the grid (<Kbd>⌥</Kbd> to place them freely).
- Right-click a section ▸ **Select section** to select it as a range across every track.

## Working on the range

| Command | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Duplicate time** | <Kbd>⌘ ⇧ D</Kbd> | Inserts a copy of the range right after it. Everything later — on every track, automation included — moves right; clips that cross the point are cut. Press again to repeat the copy. |
| **Insert silence** | <Kbd>⌘ ⇧ I</Kbd> | Opens an empty gap as long as the range at its start, pushing everything from there right. |
| **Duplicate** | <Kbd>⌘ D</Kbd> | Copies the range's material on the selected tracks right after it. Nothing moves to make room. |
| **Split** | <Kbd>⌘ E</Kbd> | Cuts the clips at the range edges (without a range: at the playhead). |
| **Consolidate** | <Kbd>⌘ J</Kbd> | Replaces the material in the range with one clip per track — notes merged, audio rendered with its gain, pitch, warp and envelopes. |
| **Loop** | <Kbd>⌘ L</Kbd> | Sets the loop to the range and turns it on. |
| **Delete** | <Kbd>Delete</Kbd> | Deletes the material in the range, cutting clips at its edges. The gap stays. |
| **Paste Bounced Audio** | <Kbd>⌘ ⇧ V</Kbd> | Renders the latest range of an audio or instrument track through its whole chain (pre-fader) and pastes it as an audio clip onto the selected audio track at the playhead, over what's there. |

**Duplicate time** and **Insert silence** act on the whole song, not only the selected tracks —
they're the way to add a bar or a chorus without shifting every track by hand. They're also in
the **Edit** menu and in the right-click menu of the range.

<Aside type="tip">
To repeat a chorus: right-click its section ▸ **Select section**, then <Kbd>⌘ ⇧ D</Kbd>. Or
right-click the section ▸ **Duplicate**.
</Aside>
