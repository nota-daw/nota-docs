Automation makes a value move over time — a filter opening up, a fade, a send swelling into a
drop. Any knob or fader in Nota can be automated: track volume and pan, every parameter of a
built-in device, plug-in parameters, MIDI effects.

<Shot id="editing/automation-lanes" alt="Automation mode in the Arrangement: the Lead track's volume lane with a curve across the song" />

## Showing the lanes

Click the **automation** button in the transport bar (<Kbd>⌘ ⇧ A</Kbd>). Each track's lane shows
one parameter, picked with the pill at the start of the lane — click it and choose from the
list. Parameters that already carry automation are marked with a brass dot and listed first.

While automation mode is on, touching any knob or fader of a track — on a device, a plug-in or
the mixer — switches that track's lane to it.

## Drawing

| Do this | To |
|---|---|
| Double-click the lane | Add a point (also between two points) |
| Drag a point | Move it |
| Double-click a point | Delete it |
| <Kbd>⌥</Kbd>-drag a segment | Bend it into a curve |
| <Kbd>⇧</Kbd>-drag | Select a range of the lane |
| <Kbd>⌘ C</Kbd> <Kbd>⌘ X</Kbd> <Kbd>⌘ V</Kbd> · <Kbd>⌘ D</Kbd> | Copy, cut, paste · duplicate the selected range |

A single click moves the playhead, as anywhere else in the Arrangement. Right-click the lane
for **Copy**, **Cut**, **Paste** and **Delete automation**.

## Recording automation

There are no modes to set. Press **Record** and move a control: its lane records what you do.
A control you move with the mouse stops writing when you let go; a hardware knob, fader, stick
or trigger keeps writing its last value until the transport stops.

## Taking over by hand

Automation always plays back. If you move an automated control **while not recording**, your
hand wins: that lane is overridden and stops pulling the knob back. The button next to the
automation button lights up while anything is overridden — click it to hand every lane back to
playback.

## Automation moves with the clips

When you move clips, the automation under them moves along. Moved to **another track**, a clip
takes only its Volume and Pan automation — device automation stays on the track it was written
for. **Edit ▸ Lock Envelopes** pins all automation to the timeline instead, so you can rearrange
clips without dragging it along.

## Other kinds of envelope

- The **master volume** has its own lane on the Master row — click it to add points.
- A **MIDI clip** has Velocity and Volume envelopes inside the clip — see
  [Piano roll](../piano-roll/#envelopes).
- An **audio clip** has Volume and Pan envelopes and an ADSR — see
  [Audio clips](../audio-clips/#envelope).

<Aside type="tip">
Lanes follow their device: reorder or remove devices and the automation stays with the
parameter it belongs to.
</Aside>
