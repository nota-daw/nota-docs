Right-click an audio clip in the Arrangement ▸ **Convert**:

| Command | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Melody to new MIDI track** | The tune — a single line of notes — on a new Nota Volt track. Best on clean single-note lines and vocals. Needs the AI model (below). |
| **Harmony to new MIDI track** | The chords and notes played together, on a new Nota Volt track. Best on sustained chords and pads. Needs the AI model (below). |
| **Drums to new MIDI track** | A new Drum Rack with a pad for each kind of hit it hears (kick, snare, hats…), each voiced by its strongest hit, and a MIDI clip that plays them where they were. |
| **Slice to new MIDI track** | The clip cut at its hits (or into 16 even slices) onto Drum Rack pads from C1 up, with a MIDI clip that plays the slices back in order. |

The MIDI clip sits under the original clip, in time with it; the status line says how many
notes or pads it made. Conversion runs in the background with a progress window you can cancel.

## Melody and harmony need the AI model

**Melody** and **Harmony** run on the **basic-pitch** AI model, which hears every note, chords
included. Until it is installed, the **Convert** menu shows **Get the AI model for melody and
harmony…** in their place; once it is in, the two commands appear (no restart). The model runs on
your computer: nothing is uploaded. **Drums** and **Slice** don't need it. You can remove it
later in [Settings ▸ Downloads ▸ AI models](../../ai/models/).

<Aside type="tip">
Clean, solo parts convert best. For a full mix, first split it with
[Separate Stems](../../ai/separate-stems/), then convert the stem you want.
</Aside>
