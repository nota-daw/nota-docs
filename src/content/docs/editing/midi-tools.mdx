Right-click an audio clip in the Arrangement ▸ **Convert**:

| Command | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Convert Melody to New MIDI Track** | The tune — a single line of notes — on a new Nota Synth track. Best on clean single-note lines and vocals. |
| **Convert Harmony to New MIDI Track** | The chords and notes played together, on a new Nota Synth track. Best on sustained chords and pads. |
| **Convert Drums to New MIDI Track** | A new Drum Rack with a pad for each kind of hit it hears (kick, snare, hats…), each voiced by its strongest hit, and a MIDI clip that plays them where they were. |
| **Slice to New MIDI Track** | The clip cut at its hits (or into 16 even slices) onto Drum Rack pads from C1 up, with a MIDI clip that plays the slices back in order. |

The MIDI clip sits under the original clip, in time with it; the status line says how many
notes or pads it made. Conversion runs in the background with a progress window you can cancel.

## Better melody and harmony with the AI model

**Convert Melody** and **Convert Harmony** work out of the box, and get noticeably better with
the **basic-pitch** AI model, which also hears chords. The menu offers it — **Get the AI Model for
Melody & Harmony…** — and it then runs on your computer: nothing is uploaded. You can remove it
later in [Settings ▸ Downloads ▸ AI Models](../../ai/models/).

<Aside type="tip">
Clean, solo parts convert best. For a full mix, first split it with
[Separate Stems](../../ai/separate-stems/), then convert the stem you want.
</Aside>
